The research focuses on two key issues: limited detection field of view and information degradation caused by occlusion. The light field propagation law is combined with artificial intelligence reconstruction to achieve field of view expansion and high-fidelity recovery of occluded targets under limited detection resources. This provides technical support for the expansion of terahertz imaging to large targets and complex detection scenarios. According to reports, terahertz digital holographic imaging has both penetration detection capabilities and amplitude and phase recovery capabilities, and has important application potential in fields such as industrial non-destructive testing and safety inspections. However, moving from the laboratory to actual measurement still faces the dual challenges of "seeing widely" and "seeing clearly": the size of the detector limits the imaging field of view, and the absorption, scattering and wavefront distortion caused by the blocking medium reduce the reconstruction quality of target details and phase information.
