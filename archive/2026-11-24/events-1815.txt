EN: China News Service, November 24. According to the website of the State Administration for Market Regulation, the State Administration for Market Regulation (National Standards Committee) recently approved the release of the national standard "Specifications for Integrated Rehabilitation and Education Services for Community Disabled Children" (GB/T 48555-2026) to provide scientific and standardized guidance for rehabilitation education services for community disabled children. There are approximately 8.17 million children with disabilities in our country, many of whom live in the community and are cared for by their families.
