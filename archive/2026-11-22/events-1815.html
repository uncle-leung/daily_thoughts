1. 青海德令哈5.6级地震 青藏铁路部分区间限速开行
5.6-magnitude earthquake in Delingha, Qinghai; speed limits imposed on certain sections of the Qinghai-Tibet Railway

中新网西宁11月22日电(祁增蓓)记者21日从中国铁路青藏集团有限公司(以下简称“青藏集团公司”)获悉，当日18时45分，青海海西州德令哈市发生5.6级地震。地震波及青藏铁路西宁至格尔木段部分区段，截至目前，部分区间限速开行，部分列车或将晚点。为确保旅客列车安全，青藏集团公司立即启动应急预案，紧急扣停途经列车，通知管内各站对车站行车设备进行全覆盖检查，封锁区间进行设备全面巡检，并及时对准备在各站点乘车的旅客进行列车晚点答疑、情绪安抚、组织退票和服务保障工作。同时，组织工务、电务、供电等部门的职工，采取“轨道车添乘检查+徒步巡查+调阅监控视频”等方式，对有震感的区段进行“地毯式”排查，全面检查铁路线路、桥梁、隧道、涵洞、信号、供电、房屋等设备设施有无松动、损坏等情况，密切防范余震，及时消除受影响区段内的安全隐患，确保设备状况良好。

ENXining, November 22 (Xinhua) – A reporter learned from China Railway Qinghai-Tibet Group Co., Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "Qinghai-Tibet Group") on November 21 that a 5.6-magnitude earthquake struck Delingha City, Haixi Prefecture, Qinghai Province, at 6:45 p.m. on the same day. The earthquake affected certain sections of the Qinghai-Tibet Railway between Xining and Golmud. As of now, speed restrictions are in place on some sections, and certain trains may be delayed. To ensure the safety of passenger trains, Qinghai-Tibet Railway Group Co., Ltd. immediately activated its emergency response plan, urgently halted passing trains, and instructed all stations within its jurisdiction to conduct comprehensive inspections of station operating equipment. The affected sections were closed off for thorough equipment inspections. Additionally, passengers preparing to board at various stations were promptly informed about train delays, provided with emotional support, and offered assistance with ticket refunds and other services. At the same time, employees from the engineering, electrical, and power supply departments, among others, were mobilized to conduct a thorough "carpet-style" inspection of the sections that experienced the earthquake. This involved methods such as "railcar inspections + foot patrols + reviewing surveillance footage." The inspection comprehensively checked railway tracks, bridges, tunnels, culverts, signals, power supply, buildings, and other equipment and facilities for any signs of loosening or damage. The aim was to closely monitor for aftershocks, promptly address any safety hazards in the affected sections, and ensure that all equipment remained in good condition.

10. 新字头、新读音 铁路部门解答车次编排改革热点问题
New train prefixes, new pronunciations: The railway authorities answer key questions about the train scheduling reform

中新网11月22日电 据“中国铁路”微信公众号消息，近期，全国铁路实施列车车次编排优化改革。针对广大旅客关注的T、K字头用途变更，普速列车Z、N、V新字头的选用、读音调整、出行保障等热点问题，铁路部门发布权威解答。为什么将T、K字头调整至高速旅客列车车次编排使用？K、T作为旅客列车车次字头字母，分别于1997年、2000年全国铁路大提速期间首次采用，意为“快速”“特快”，代表当时速度等级最高的旅客列车，最高运行时速可达140公里。

ENChina News Service, November 22 – According to the "China Railway" WeChat official account, the national railway system has recently implemented a reform to optimize train scheduling. In response to hot topics that passengers are concerned about, such as the change in the use of the prefixes "T" and "K," the selection of the new prefixes "Z," "N," and "V" for conventional trains, adjustments to their pronunciation, and travel guarantees, the railway authorities have issued authoritative answers. Why have the T and K prefixes been reassigned for use in the scheduling of high-speed passenger trains? The letters K and T, used as prefixes for passenger train numbers, were first introduced in 1997 and 2000, respectively, during the nationwide railway speed-up campaign. They stood for "fast" and "express," representing the fastest passenger trains of that time, with maximum operating speeds of up to 140 kilometers per hour.

ENPhnom Penh, November 22 (Xinhua) – The Cambodian Ministry of Health announced on November 22 local time that a confirmed case of mpox has recently been detected in the country, and the patient is currently receiving treatment in a hospital. In its announcement, the Cambodian Ministry of Health stated that the aforementioned case is the first to be reported in Cambodia after no new cases of mpox infection had been recorded in the past two and a half years. Between December 12, 2023, and May 14, 2024, Cambodia reported a total of 20 confirmed cases of mpox. The Cambodian Ministry of Health noted that mpox is primarily transmitted between people through close contact, including direct contact with the affected areas of the patient's skin, bodily fluids, and respiratory secretions.
