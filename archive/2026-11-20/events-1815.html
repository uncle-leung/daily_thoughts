Data show that rising energy prices are an important factor driving upward prices. With the withdrawal of government subsidies, electricity prices increased from a 2.4% year-on-year decrease in September to a year-on-year increase of 1.6% in October. 中新社东京11月20日电(记者 朱晨曦)日本多个民间团体20日向防卫省递交由约4.7万名日本民众联合签名的材料，反对日本拥有核动力潜艇，要求政府撤回相关计划。此次联合签名活动由“日本原子力资料情报室”等民间机构和组织共同发起。签名活动声明中说，日本作为唯一遭受过原子弹轰炸的国家，一直宣称有责任推动废除核武器，而拥有核动力潜艇无异于主动背弃这一承诺。声明表示，强烈要求政府撤回拥有核潜艇的相关计划。

ENChina News Service, Tokyo, November 20 (Reporter Zhu Chenxi) On the 20th, multiple Japanese civil groups submitted materials signed by about 47,000 Japanese people to the Ministry of Defense, opposing Japan's possession of nuclear-powered submarines and demanding that the government withdraw related plans. This joint signature event was co-sponsored by private institutions and organizations such as the "Japan Atomic Energy Information Office". The signature event statement stated that Japan, as the only country that has suffered atomic bombing, has always claimed its responsibility to promote the abolition of nuclear weapons, and possessing nuclear-powered submarines is tantamount to actively breaking this commitment. The statement stated that it strongly urged the government to withdraw related plans to possess nuclear submarines.

中新社圣保罗11月19日电 巴西农业部19日发布数据显示，2026年巴西农牧业总产值预计达1.46万亿雷亚尔，较上年创下的历史纪录下降2.6%。尽管受不利气候条件影响，农牧业产值有所回落，但巴西农产品出口继续保持增长势头。数据显示，受降雨过量及阶段性干旱影响，今年巴西种植业产值预计同比下降3.9%，畜牧业下降0.2%。其中，大豆和肉牛养殖业表现较好，产值预计分别增长4.6%和11.8%。

ENChina News Service, Sao Paulo, November 19. Data released by the Brazilian Ministry of Agriculture on the 19th showed that the total output value of Brazil's agriculture and animal husbandry in 2026 is expected to reach 1.46 trillion reais, a 2.6% decrease from the historical record set last year. Although the output value of agriculture and animal husbandry has declined due to adverse weather conditions, Brazil's agricultural exports continue to maintain growth momentum. Data show that due to excessive rainfall and periodic drought, the output value of Brazil's planting industry is expected to drop by 3.9% year-on-year this year, and the livestock industry will drop by 0.2%. Among them, the soybean and beef cattle breeding industries performed better, with output values ​​expected to increase by 4.6% and 11.8% respectively.

9. Anthropic上市 创史上最大IPO融资纪录
Anthropic goes public, setting record for largest IPO financing in history

中新社纽约11月19日电 (记者 王帆)美国人工智能公司Anthropic当地时间19日在纽约纳斯达克证券交易所上市，其首次公开募股(IPO)融资总额超过813亿美元，创下美股史上最大IPO融资规模纪录。当天，Anthropic以每股83美元的发行价挂牌，计入私募配售等股份后，完全稀释市值约为1.5万亿美元。正式开盘时，该股报111.50美元，公司市值一度达到约2万亿美元。截至收盘，该股报97.17美元，涨幅17.07%。

ENChina News Service, New York, November 19 (Reporter Wang Fan) Anthropic, an American artificial intelligence company, was listed on the Nasdaq Stock Exchange in New York on the 19th local time. Its initial public offering (IPO) total financing exceeded US$81.3 billion, setting a record for the largest IPO financing scale in the history of the U.S. stock market. On that day, Anthropic was listed at an issue price of US$83 per share. After including private placements and other shares, the fully diluted market value was approximately US$1.5 trillion. When the market officially opened, the stock was trading at $111.50, and the company's market value once reached about $2 trillion. As of the close, the stock was trading at $97.17, an increase of 17.07%.

10. 普京：克里米亚大桥遭袭后 不再讨论海上停火
Putin: No more talks on a maritime ceasefire after the attack on the Crimean Bridge

新华社莫斯科11月19日电 俄罗斯总统普京19日表示，在克里米亚大桥遭袭后，俄方不会就海上停火或停止攻击能源设施与有关方面进行谈判。据俄罗斯总统网站19日发布的消息，在回应记者关于克里米亚大桥近日多次遭袭及受损情况的提问时，普京表示，克里米亚大桥是民用设施，俄方从未通过该桥运输军事装备和弹药，也没有通过该桥运输石油及石油制品。普京说，俄方此前曾评估与有关方面就海上停火及停止攻击能源设施举行谈判的可能性，但克里米亚大桥再次遭袭后，俄方不会就此进行谈判。他表示，俄方将作出回应，且力度将比乌方对克里米亚大桥的攻击更大。

ENXinhua News Agency, Moscow, November 19 – Russian President Vladimir Putin stated on November 19 that, following the attack on the Crimean Bridge, Russia will not engage in negotiations with relevant parties regarding a maritime ceasefire or the cessation of attacks on energy facilities. According to information published on the Russian Presidential website on November 19, in response to a reporter's question about the recent multiple attacks on and damage to the Crimean Bridge, Putin stated that the Crimean Bridge is a civilian facility, and Russia has never used the bridge to transport military equipment and ammunition, nor has it used the bridge to transport oil and petroleum products. Putin stated that Russia had previously considered the possibility of holding negotiations with relevant parties regarding a maritime ceasefire and an end to attacks on energy facilities. However, following the renewed attack on the Crimean Bridge, Russia will not engage in such negotiations. He stated that Russia would respond, and that its response would be more forceful than Ukraine's attack on the Crimean Bridge.
