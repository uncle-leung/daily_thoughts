One light-day refers to the distance light travels in 24 hours, which is approximately 26 billion kilometers. In a statement, NASA said that the Voyager 1 probe reached a point one light-day away from Earth at 5:16 a.m. Eastern Time on November 18, making it the farthest-traveling spacecraft in human history. NASA confirmed at 5:16 a.m. Eastern Time on November 19 that it had received a signal from the Voyager 1 probe, which had been sent a day earlier.
