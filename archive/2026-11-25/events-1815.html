1. 台气象部门：台湾东南部海域发生5.5级地震
Taiwan Meteorological Department: A 5.5-magnitude earthquake occurred in the southeastern waters of Taiwan

中新社台北11月25日电 据台湾气象部门消息，台湾东南部海域25日16时47分发生5.5级地震。台东县兰屿等地有明显震感。综合中央社、中时新闻网等台媒报道，此次地震震源深度为16.7公里，震中位于台东县政府南南东方向101.9公里处。台东县兰屿最大震度4级，屏东县鹅銮鼻、满州、恒春以及高雄市旗山最大震度2级。

ENChina News Service, Taipei, November 25. According to the Taiwan Meteorological Department, a 5.5-magnitude earthquake occurred in the southeastern waters of Taiwan at 16:47 on the 25th. The earthquake was clearly felt in Lanyu, Taitung County and other places. According to reports from the Central News Agency, China Times News Network and other Taiwanese media, the focal depth of the earthquake was 16.7 kilometers, and the epicenter was located 101.9 kilometers south-southeast of the Taitung County Government. The maximum magnitude of the earthquake was magnitude 4 in Lanyu, Taitung County, and magnitude 2 in Eluanbi, Manzhou, Hengchun, Pingtung County, and Qishan, Kaohsiung City.

2. 中方呼吁申办《海洋生物多样性协定》秘书处相关国家友好合作、合理竞争
China calls on countries involved in the bid to host the Secretariat of the Agreement on Marine Biodiversity to engage in friendly cooperation and reasonable competition.

中新社北京11月25日电 (记者 周昕)中国外交部申办《海洋生物多样性协定》(以下简称《协定》)秘书处特使易先良25日在北京表示，有关国家不应把《协定》政治化，要在相关规则的前提下，友好合作、合理竞争，共同为海洋治理与发展作出贡献。当日，中国公共外交协会举办“临甲7号沙龙”专题吹风会，围绕“践行多边主义 完善全球海洋治理”主题向中外媒体吹风。针对近日美国国务院支持比利时和智利合并申办《海洋生物多样性协定》秘书处的声明，易先良表示，在未同广大《协定》缔约方协商的情况下，比利时和智利宣布合并申办秘书处，此举存在不符合程序正义、不符合国际惯例、不符合公平竞争等诸多问题，引发国际社会的广泛讨论和质疑。易先良说，美国并非《协定》缔约方，在《协定》秘书处选址问题上没有发言权。

ENChina News Service, Beijing, November 25 (Reporter Zhou Xin) Yi Xianliang, special envoy of the Secretariat of the Chinese Ministry of Foreign Affairs’ bid to host the Agreement on Marine Biodiversity (hereinafter referred to as the Agreement), said in Beijing on the 25th that relevant countries should not politicize the Agreement and should engage in friendly cooperation and reasonable competition under the premise of relevant rules to jointly contribute to ocean governance and development. On the same day, the China Public Diplomacy Association held a special briefing titled "Linjia Salon No. 7" to brief Chinese and foreign media on the theme of "Practice Multilateralism and Improve Global Ocean Governance." In response to the recent statement of the U.S. State Department supporting Belgium and Chile’s merger bid to host the Secretariat of the Agreement on Marine Biological Diversity, Yi Xianliang said that without consulting the majority of parties to the Agreement, Belgium and Chile announced their merger bid to host the Secretariat. This move has many problems such as being inconsistent with procedural justice, international practice, and fair competition, and has triggered extensive discussions and doubts in the international community. Yi Xianliang said that the United States is not a party to the Agreement and has no say in the location of the Agreement Secretariat.
