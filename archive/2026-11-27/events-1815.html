1. 中国驻日本大使馆再次提醒中国公民注意防范地震等自然灾害
The Chinese Embassy in Japan once again reminds Chinese citizens to take precautions against natural disasters such as earthquakes.

中新网11月27日电 据“中国驻日本大使馆”微信公众号消息，近期，日本熊本县、茨城县、千叶县、静冈县、北海道近海等地地震频发。日政府于11月2日正式成立防灾厅，以应对可能发生的首都直下型地震、南海海槽地震等大规模自然灾害。中国驻日本大使馆再次提醒在日中国公民注意防范地震等自然灾害：。一、切实增强防范意识，对老旧住宅进行抗震性能检查，对书柜、冰箱、大型电器等家具进行防震加固，提前了解住所、工作地等经常出入场所是否面临地震、海啸等风险，熟悉附近避难所地址和逃生路线。

ENChina News Service, November 27 – According to the WeChat official account of the "Chinese Embassy in Japan," earthquakes have been occurring frequently in recent times in areas such as Kumamoto Prefecture, Ibaraki Prefecture, Chiba Prefecture, Shizuoka Prefecture, and the waters off Hokkaido in Japan. On November 2, the Japanese government officially established the Disaster Management Agency to respond to potential large-scale natural disasters such as a direct earthquake under the capital and a Nankai Trough earthquake. The Chinese Embassy in Japan once again reminds Chinese citizens in Japan to take precautions against natural disasters such as earthquakes: 1. Effectively enhance awareness of preparedness. Conduct seismic performance inspections of old residences. Reinforce bookshelves, refrigerators, large appliances, and other furniture to withstand earthquakes. Determine in advance whether places you frequently visit, such as your residence and workplace, are at risk of earthquakes, tsunamis, etc. Familiarize yourself with the locations of nearby shelters and escape routes.

ENChina News Service, Brussels, November 27 (Reporter De Yongjian) Eurostat released the EU's import and export data for goods trade in the third quarter of this year on the 26th, showing that the EU's total imports of goods trade in the third quarter of this year were 730 billion euros. China is the largest source of imports for the EU's goods trade. Data show that the EU's total imports of goods trade in the third quarter of this year were 730 billion euros, an increase of 1.7% from the second quarter of this year and an increase of 15.7% from the third quarter of 2025. In terms of major trading partners, China was the EU's largest import source of goods trade in the third quarter of this year, with a total import of 163 billion euros from China, accounting for 22.3% of the EU's total imports of goods trade in the quarter. The United States is the EU's second largest import source of goods trade, with a total import of 100.9 billion euros from the United States, accounting for 13.8% of the EU's total imports of goods trade in the quarter.
