3. 《生态质量评价标准（试行）》将于今年12月1日起实施
EN: The "Ecological Quality Assessment Standards (Trial)" will come into effect on December 1st of this year.

EN: China News Service, November 27 – On November 27, the Ministry of Ecology and Environment held its regular November press conference. Ministry of Ecology and Environment spokesperson Pei Xiaofei announced that the Ministry of Ecology and Environment recently released the "Ecological Quality Assessment Standards (Trial)," which will come into effect on December 1 of this year. Pei Xiaofei stated that in order to assess the state of regional ecological quality and the effectiveness of conservation efforts, the Ministry of Ecology and Environment recently issued the "Ecological Quality Assessment Standards (Trial)" (hereinafter referred to as the "Standards"), which will come into effect on December 1 of this year. The release of the Standard will further improve the regulatory system for ecological protection and restoration, providing technical support for the performance evaluation of the Beautiful China initiative. The "Standards" are closely aligned with the strategic requirements of enhancing the diversity, stability, and sustainability of ecosystems. In terms of the indicator system, they integrate and optimize the "Technical Guidelines for Ecological and Environmental Status Assessment," the "Regional Biodiversity Assessment Standards," and the "Regional Ecological Quality Assessment Methods (Trial)." They comprehensively consider factors such as data availability, regional differences, and interannual variations. From the three aspects of ecosystem diversity, stability, and sustainability, they establish eight basic indicators, including the proportion of natural ecosystem area, habitat connectivity, and the intensity of human activity impact on ecological spaces.
