EN: Newly discovered Homo erectus fossils in Guangxi, China, will help solve key mysteries of human evolution
中新网北京11月19日电 (记者 孙自法)直立人是生存年代最长的古人类物种之一，已知在约190万年到10.8万年前分布在整个非洲到欧亚大陆东部。不过，由于此前中国特别是华南地区相关化石记录存在空白，成为古人类演化关键谜题之一，备受关注。中外科学家最近在广西相距15公里的两处洞穴遗址中鉴定出8颗直立人牙齿化石，发现该种群曾生存于距今32.4万年至18.4万年的雨林环境中。这既表明直立人在中国的存续年代比之前的认识至少后推了8万年，也挑战了关于直立人群体无法适应雨林环境行为局限的假设。
EN: Beijing, November 19 (Xinhua) – Homo erectus is one of the longest-lived species of early humans, known to have inhabited areas from across Africa to the eastern part of Eurasia between approximately 1.9 million and 108,000 years ago. However, due to the previous lack of relevant fossil records in China, particularly in South China, this has become one of the key mysteries in human evolution and has attracted considerable attention. Recently, Chinese and foreign scientists identified eight Homo erectus tooth fossils at two cave sites in Guangxi, 15 kilometers apart. They discovered that this population lived in a rainforest environment between 324,000 and 184,000 years ago. This not only indicates that Homo erectus existed in China at least 80,000 years later than previously thought, but also challenges the assumption that Homo erectus populations were behaviorally limited and unable to adapt to rainforest environments.
