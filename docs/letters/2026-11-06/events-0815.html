ENChina News Service, Beijing, November 6 (Reporter Xie Yanbing) The 25th Session of the Standing Committee of the 14th National People's Congress voted to pass the Anti-Cross-Border Corruption Law on November 6. The law has 6 chapters and 47 articles, including mechanisms and responsibilities, case handling and international cooperation, integrity and compliance, legal responsibilities, etc. The Anti-Cross-Border Corruption Law is formulated to prevent and punish cross-border corruption, deepen international cooperation in anti-corruption, safeguard national sovereignty, security, and development interests, and promote the construction of a fair and reasonable anti-corruption global governance system. The law stipulates that anti-cross-border corruption work adheres to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, adheres to the overall national security concept, adheres to no restricted areas, full coverage, and zero tolerance in anti-corruption, adheres to addressing both the symptoms and root causes, and implements systematic governance, promotes the integrated promotion of those who dare not corrupt, cannot corrupt, and do not want to corrupt, and insist on actively participating in global anti-corruption governance.
