1. 我国南极活动与环境保护法出台 对国际南极治理意义几何？
What is the significance of China’s Antarctic Activities and Environmental Protection Law on international Antarctic governance?

十四届全国人大常委会第二十五次会议今天(6日)表决通过《中华人民共和国南极活动与环境保护法》，明年1月1日起施行。该法的通过，是我国严格遵守、切实履行国际南极治理的基石——《南极条约》的具体体现。它对外是一部履约法，对内则全面系统地将我国南极工作实践经验上升为法律，让各类南极活动有法可依。南极，作为地球上最为纯净的大陆，是触摸全球气候脉搏的窗口，是地球生态“过去”与“未来”的交汇之处。

ENThe 25th Session of the Standing Committee of the 14th National People's Congress voted today (6th) to pass the "Antarctic Activities and Environmental Protection Law of the People's Republic of China", which will come into effect on January 1 next year. The passage of this law is a concrete manifestation of China's strict compliance with and effective implementation of the Antarctic Treaty, the cornerstone of international Antarctic governance. Externally, it is a contract fulfillment law, and internally, it comprehensively and systematically elevates China's practical experience in Antarctic work into law, so that all types of Antarctic activities have laws to follow. Antarctica, as the purest continent on earth, is a window to touch the pulse of global climate and the intersection of the "past" and "future" of the earth's ecology.

2. 我国首个海山数字化空间地理信息成果发布
my country's first seamount digital spatial geographical information results released

中新网厦门11月6日电 (记者 阮煜琳)由自然资源部中国大洋事务管理局、国家海洋信息中心联合打造“海山图志(Seamount Atlas)”日前在厦门国际海洋周开幕式上面向全球发布，这是我国首个海山数字化空间地理信息成果，标志着我国海山空间信息建设取得新进展。据了解，图志不仅是我国长期深海调查成果的系统整编，也是中国积极参与全球海洋治理的重要成果。全球海山数量庞大，现有研究估计可能达到数万至十万座以上，但获得国际标准化名称的仅是其中少数。我国通过持续开展大洋科学调查，发现并命名54座海山，名称经国际海底地名分委会(SCUFN)审议通过，纳入国际海底地名录，为全球海底地理实体标准化识别作出了中国贡献。

ENChina News Service, Xiamen, November 6 (Reporter Ruan Yulin) The "Seamount Atlas" jointly created by the China Ocean Affairs Administration of the Ministry of Natural Resources and the National Ocean Information Center was recently released to the world at the opening ceremony of the Xiamen International Ocean Week. This is China's first seamount digital spatial geographical information achievement, marking new progress in China's seamount spatial information construction. It is understood that the map is not only a systematic compilation of China's long-term deep-sea survey results, but also an important result of China's active participation in global ocean governance. There are a huge number of seamounts in the world. Existing research estimates that there may be tens to more than 100,000 seamounts. However, only a few of them have received internationally standardized names. Through continuous oceanographic scientific surveys, China has discovered and named 54 seamounts. The names have been reviewed and approved by the International Subcommittee on Undersea Place Names (SCUFN) and included in the international undersea place directory, making China's contribution to the standardized identification of global undersea geographical entities.

ENChina News Service, Panzhihua, November 6 (Han Jinyu) "I had never heard of the city of Panzhihua before. When I came to the museum and saw those old photos and listened to the explanations, I realized that the third-line construction was not easy and that Panzhihua was born like this." At the China Third-line Construction Museum in Panzhihua, Taiwanese self-media person Guo Renrong said. On November 6, the 2026 Taiwanese self-media people's Tianfu Travels·Panxi Journey event entered Panzhihua City, Sichuan Province. Nearly 20 self-media and media reporters from both sides of the Taiwan Strait visited Panzhihua China's Third Front Construction Museum, Ashuda Four Seasons Flower Sea Scenic Area and other places to look back at the history of third front construction and feel the city's historical heritage and ecological changes. It is understood that the China Third Front Construction Museum in Panzhihua is the only Third Front Construction themed museum in the country that has been approved by the State Council to be named "China". It contains more than 26,000 pieces (sets) of cultural relics, more than 30,000 pictures, and more than 8,000 minutes of oral history videos. It is currently the largest Third Front Construction themed museum in China with the most comprehensive exhibitions and the largest collection, showing the magnificent history of Third Front Construction.
