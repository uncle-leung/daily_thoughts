ENOn November 24, 2026, the Intermediate People's Court of Zibo City, Shandong Province publicly pronounced the first instance verdict on Wu Shenghua, former member of the Standing Committee of the Guizhou Provincial Party Committee and former secretary of the Bijie Municipal Party Committee, in the bribery case. The defendant Wu Shenghua was sentenced to life imprisonment for accepting bribes, deprived of political rights for life, and confiscated all personal property; the property and interest obtained from bribery by Wu Shenghua, who was recovered, were turned over to the state treasury in accordance with the law, and the remaining part continued to be recovered. The Zibo Intermediate People's Court held that defendant Wu Shenghua's behavior constituted the crime of accepting bribes. The amount of bribes Wu Shenghua accepted was extremely huge and caused extremely heavy losses to the interests of the country and the people, so he should be punished in accordance with the law.

6. 欧盟“点名”5个成员国存在经济失衡风险

中新社布鲁塞尔11月24日电 (记者 德永健)欧盟24日依据“欧洲学期”机制发布成员国宏观经济预警报告，认定意大利、匈牙利、斯洛伐克、罗马尼亚和保加利亚存在经济失衡风险，将于2027年上半年对5国进行深入审查。这份预警报告当日随欧盟委员会“秋季学期”报告发布。自2011年启动“欧洲学期”机制，欧盟每年都会按“学期”发布一揽子报告和建议，以此监督指导成员国宏观经济政策，谨防出现主权债务危机等重大风险。预警报告显示，欧盟委员会认定意大利、匈牙利和斯洛伐克存在经济失衡风险，罗马尼亚存在经济过度失衡风险，2027年上半年将对4国进行深入审查。

ENChina News Service, Brussels, November 24 (Reporter Toku Yongjian) The European Union issued a macroeconomic warning report for member states on the 24th based on the "European Semester" mechanism, identifying Italy, Hungary, Slovakia, Romania and Bulgaria as having risks of economic imbalance, and will conduct in-depth reviews of the five countries in the first half of 2027. This early warning report was released on the same day as the European Commission's "Autumn Semester" report. Since the launch of the "European Semester" mechanism in 2011, the EU has released a package of reports and recommendations every "semester" to supervise and guide the macroeconomic policies of member states and guard against major risks such as sovereign debt crises. The early warning report shows that the European Commission has determined that Italy, Hungary and Slovakia are at risk of economic imbalance, and Romania is at risk of excessive economic imbalance. In-depth reviews will be conducted on the four countries in the first half of 2027.
