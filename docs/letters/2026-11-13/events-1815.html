ENChina News Service, Beijing, November 13 (Reporter Li Chun) China's National Health Commission held a press conference in Beijing on the 13th. National Health Commission spokesperson Yang Jinrui pointed out that China strictly prohibits any form of surrogacy, and organizing surrogacy is illegal and criminal. At the press conference that day, Yang Jinrui said that recently, there have been media reports that illegal intermediaries carry out "underground surrogacy" in places without medical conditions, and are carried out by unqualified non-professionals. The operation process is extremely irregular and the risk is extremely high. These illegal institutions often abuse ovulation induction drugs, which can easily cause ovarian damage to women, cause massive bleeding and serious infections, and even endanger their lives. At the same time, the conditions for embryo culture and transplantation are poor, which can easily cause neonatal miscarriage, premature birth, stillbirth, and severe birth defects. Yang Jinrui said that the National Health Commission reminded that any form of surrogacy is strictly prohibited in China.
