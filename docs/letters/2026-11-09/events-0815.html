1. 南非公布地方选举结果 过半数市镇现“悬峙议会”
South Africa announces local election results, more than half of towns now have hung parliaments

中新社约翰内斯堡11月8日电 (记者 孙翔)南非独立选举委员会8日晚公布2026年地方政府选举结果，执政党非洲人国民大会(简称非国大)得票率由2021年的45.59%降至30.76%，但仍位居首位。南非全国超过半数市镇议会出现“悬峙议会”，即没有任何政党获得绝对多数席位，地方政府组建面临多党谈判。根据公布结果，民主联盟以24.64%的得票率位居第二，民族之矛党以9.76%位居第三。非国大在53个市镇获得绝对多数席位，民主联盟在12个市镇获得绝对多数席位。

ENChina News Service, Johannesburg, November 8 (Reporter Sun Xiang) The Independent Electoral Commission of South Africa announced the results of the 2026 local government election on the evening of the 8th. The voting rate of the ruling party African National Congress (ANC) dropped from 45.59% in 2021 to 30.76%, but it still ranked first. More than half of the town councils in South Africa have "hung councils", which means that no political party obtains an absolute majority of seats, and the formation of local government faces multi-party negotiations. According to the announced results, the Democratic Alliance ranked second with 24.64% of the vote, and the National Spear Party ranked third with 9.76%. The ANC won an absolute majority in 53 towns and the Democratic Alliance won an absolute majority in 12 towns.

ENChina News Service, November 9. According to the website of the National Bureau of Statistics, in October 2026, the national industrial producer price increased by 5.7% year-on-year and 0.7% month-on-month. The purchasing price of industrial producers increased by 7.9% year-on-year and 1.1% month-on-month. On average from January to October, the ex-factory price of industrial producers increased by 2.7% compared with the same period last year, and the purchasing price of industrial producers increased by 4.0%. ENChina News Service, November 9. ENOpen-screen ads are too cumbersome, how to fix them? Travel navigation, parking payment, access control verification, tram charging, express delivery... Take out your mobile phone and scan the code, and everything is done at your fingertips. This is a scenario used by hundreds of millions of people every day. However, as there are more and more apps on mobile phones and they are used more and more frequently, the on-screen ads and pop-up ads that "can't be turned off" and "jump randomly" are starting to make people upset again.
