ENChina News Service, Guangzhou, November 6 (Guo Jun, Hu Haiyan) The mid-span of the Zhanglong section of the Ruimei-Meijiang Railway Meijiang Bridge, built by the Shenzhen Engineering Construction Headquarters of the Guangzhou Bureau of the State Railway and constructed by the China Railway 15th Bureau Group, was successfully closed on the 5th, marking the completion of the entire Meijiang Bridge and a milestone breakthrough in the key and difficult bridge projects in the bidding section, clearing key obstacles for subsequent girder track laying and line construction. Meijiang Super Large Bridge is a third-line bridge jointly built by Ruimei Railway and Longlong Railway. The starting and ending mileage of the bridge is DK241+228.659~DK242+158.485, the center mileage is DK241+693.555, and the total length of the bridge is 929.826 meters. This time, the continuous beams from piers 16 to 19 in Helong span the Zhanglong Railway at DK242+029.5, with an angle of 68° to the existing railway, and are close to the business line. The construction safety risks are high and the control requirements are strict. In order to minimize the disturbance of the bridge construction to the existing railway operations and ensure the safety of trains on the existing lines, the project department set up special protective scaffolding within the continuous beam operation range to build a strong safety protection barrier for the existing lines.

8. 泰国部署2027年PM2.5防控措施
Thailand deploys PM2.5 prevention and control measures in 2027

中新社曼谷11月6日电 (王茜 李映民)泰国公共卫生部6日召开2027年雾霾及细颗粒物(PM2.5)防控会议，要求加强全国40个高风险府的监测和管理，并部署五项医疗卫生应对措施，重点保护约1600万人的脆弱群体。泰国公共卫生部数据显示，2024年至2026年，全国超过60个府出现PM2.5浓度超标，其中40个府连续三年超过每立方米37.5微克的标准。卫生部门将加强高风险地区污染监测，提前做好医疗服务和应急准备。会议提出的五项措施包括加强医疗服务体系建设、完善高风险地区监测、建立脆弱群体风险地图和信息登记系统、整合地方财政资源，以及强化中央与地方卫生机构之间的协调合作。

ENChina News Service, Bangkok, November 6 (Wang Qi, Li Yingmin) Thailand's Ministry of Public Health held a 2027 haze and fine particulate matter (PM2.5) prevention and control meeting on the 6th, requiring the strengthening of monitoring and management of 40 high-risk provinces across the country, and the deployment of five medical and health response measures to focus on protecting vulnerable groups of approximately 16 million people. Data from Thailand's Ministry of Public Health shows that from 2024 to 2026, more than 60 provinces across the country have exceeded PM2.5 concentration standards, of which 40 have exceeded the standard of 37.5 micrograms per cubic meter for three consecutive years. The health department will strengthen pollution monitoring in high-risk areas and prepare medical services and emergency preparedness in advance. The five measures proposed at the meeting include strengthening the construction of the medical service system, improving monitoring of high-risk areas, establishing a risk map and information registration system for vulnerable groups, integrating local financial resources, and strengthening coordination and cooperation between central and local health agencies.
