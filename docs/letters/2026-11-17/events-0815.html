7. 日本2026年三季度GDP再现负增长
Japan’s GDP will see negative growth again in the third quarter of 2026

中新社东京11月16日电 (记者 朱晨曦)日本内阁府16日发表的初步统计结果显示，受个人消费下滑影响，扣除物价变动因素，日本2026年第三季度实际国内生产总值(GDP)环比基本持平，按年率计算萎缩0.1%，自2025年第三季度以来再次出现负增长。据日本共同社报道，由于物价持续上涨，加之暴雨与台风等灾害也成为拖累因素，导致今年第三季度个人消费表现疲软。数据显示，内需对当季日本经济增长的贡献为0.6个百分点。当季占日本经济比重一半以上的个人消费环比下降0.3%，连续两个季度出现负增长；内需另一支柱企业设备投资环比增加1.6%。

ENChina News Service, Tokyo, November 16 (Reporter Zhu Chenxi) Preliminary statistical results released by the Japanese Cabinet Office on the 16th showed that due to the decline in personal consumption and excluding price changes, Japan's actual gross domestic product (GDP) in the third quarter of 2026 was basically the same month-on-month, shrinking by 0.1% on an annual basis, and has experienced negative growth again since the third quarter of 2025. According to Japan's Kyodo News Agency, personal consumption performance in the third quarter of this year was weak as prices continued to rise and disasters such as heavy rains and typhoons also became drag factors. Data show that domestic demand contributed 0.6 percentage points to Japan's economic growth in the quarter. Personal consumption, which accounts for more than half of Japan's economy, fell by 0.3% month-on-month in the quarter, showing negative growth for two consecutive quarters; corporate equipment investment, another pillar of domestic demand, increased by 1.6% month-on-month.

ENChina News Service, Antalya, Turkey, November 16 (Reporters Wei Yuan, Wang Gaofei, Chen Su) The 31st Conference of the Parties (COP31) of the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change is being held in Antalya, Turkey. Solomon said that today, ten years after the signing of the Paris Agreement, green transformation has become an unstoppable trend, but geopolitical uncertainty and governance fragmentation are still threatening the progress made by all parties. He emphasized that to achieve harmonious coexistence, we must break down the barriers between climate policy and gender equality and adopt a collaborative governance approach with gender equality as the core.
