中新社北京11月17日电 (记者 王梦瑶)中国国家邮政局17日发布的报告显示，10月中国快递发展指数为477.8，同比提升0.5%。快递业务量连续两个月超170亿件。其中，发展规模指数为623.7，同比提升1.2%；服务质量指数为705.8，同比提升0.1%；发展能力指数为246.6，同比提升2.5%；发展趋势指数为49.4。报告显示，10月，中国快递业务量预计同比略有上涨，快递业务收入预计同比增长3%左右。

ENChina News Service, Beijing, November 17 (Reporter Wang Mengyao) A report released by the State Post Bureau of China on the 17th showed that China’s express delivery development index was 477.8 in October, a year-on-year increase of 0.5%. Express delivery business volume has exceeded 17 billion pieces for two consecutive months. Among them, the development scale index was 623.7, a year-on-year increase of 1.2%; the service quality index was 705.8, a year-on-year increase of 0.1%; the development capability index was 246.6, a year-on-year increase of 2.5%; and the development trend index was 49.4. The report shows that in October, China's express delivery business volume is expected to increase slightly year-on-year, and express delivery business revenue is expected to increase by about 3% year-on-year.

Foreign media quoted official information from Yemen as saying that in recent days, the Houthi armed forces have successively launched ballistic missiles and rockets towards Aden, targeting local airports, ports and military facilities. 7. 美法官撤销国土安全部限制国际学生等停留期限新规
U.S. judge reverses Department of Homeland Security’s new rule limiting the length of stay for international students and others

中新社纽约11月16日电 美国马萨诸塞州联邦地区法院一名法官16日作出裁决，认定国土安全部此前公布的限制国际学生、交流访问者和外国媒体代表在美停留期限的新规违反《行政程序法》，决定予以撤销。马萨诸塞州联邦地区法院法官塞勒(F. Dennis Saylor)在裁决书中写道，美国国土安全部7月公布的新规拟取消F、J和I类非移民签证持有者原有的“身份期限框架”，改为设定固定停留期限。这一规定决策过程武断，缺乏合理考量，构成滥用自由裁量权。若新规实施，将进一步削弱美国吸引国际人才的能力，扰乱教育计划，并加重学校负担。

ENChina News Service, New York, November 16. A judge of the Federal District Court of Massachusetts in the United States ruled on the 16th that the new regulations previously announced by the Department of Homeland Security that limited the stay period of international students, exchange visitors, and foreign media representatives in the United States violated the Administrative Procedure Act and decided to revoke them. Judge F. Dennis Saylor of the Federal District Court of Massachusetts wrote in the ruling that the new regulations announced by the U.S. Department of Homeland Security in July intend to cancel the original "status period framework" for F, J and I non-immigrant visa holders and instead set a fixed period of stay. The decision-making process under this provision is arbitrary and lacks reasonable consideration, constituting an abuse of discretion. If implemented, the new rules will further weaken the United States' ability to attract international talent, disrupt education programs, and increase the burden on schools.

ENChina News Service, Phnom Penh, November 16th (Zhang Yanlin, Zhang Anqi) Cambodian King Norodom Sihamoni and French President Macron jointly cut the ribbon on the completion of the restoration project of the ancient temple of Ximeben on the 16th local time. This thousand-year-old monument is located in Siem Reap Province, Cambodia. After a 14-year joint restoration by Cambodia and France, it is now reopened to domestic and foreign tourists. The ancient temple of Sime Beng is located in the center of Sibalai Lake in Siem Reap Province. It was built in the mid-11th century and is an important historical and cultural heritage of Cambodia. The temple launched a large-scale restoration and reconstruction project in 2012, which was jointly carried out by relevant parties in Cambodia and France. The total investment in the project was US$12 million.
