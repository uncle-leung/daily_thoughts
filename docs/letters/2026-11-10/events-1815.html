4. 中国团队研制出国际首台下一代射频超导加速器
Chinese team develops world's first next-generation radio frequency superconducting accelerator

中新社北京11月10日电 (记者 孙自法)记者10日从中国科学院近代物理研究所获悉，该所和先进能源科学与技术广东省实验室联合团队历时近十年持续攻关，成功研制出国际首台无液氦铌三锡薄膜射频超导电子加速器，并实现稳定载束。这项基于构建下一代射频超导技术体系主要突破方向铌三锡、实现加速器多领域深度交叉的重要研发成果，近日入选中国机械工程学会表面工程分会“2026中国表面科技十大进展”。研究团队介绍，铌三锡可提升超导腔运行温度，推动加速器摆脱液氦依赖，变革超导直线加速器的运行模式，是射频超导领域的前沿研究热点。该技术成果属于加速器物理、超导材料与表面工程深度交叉领域，研究团队通过关键科学与工程难题开展系统研究，揭示相关内在机理，突破系列关键工艺，研制出国际首台无液氦铌三锡薄膜射频超导电子加速器，并实现稳定载束，从而构建起从基础机理、工艺装备到样机载束试验的系统性技术体系。

ENChina News Service, Beijing, November 10 (Reporter Sun Zifa) The reporter learned from the Institute of Modern Physics of the Chinese Academy of Sciences on the 10th that the joint team of the Institute of Modern Physics and the Guangdong Provincial Laboratory of Advanced Energy Science and Technology spent nearly ten years on continuous research and successfully developed the world's first liquid-free helium-niobium-tri-tin thin film radio frequency superconducting electron accelerator, and achieved stable beam loading. This important research and development achievement based on the main breakthrough direction of building the next generation radio frequency superconducting technology system, niobium and tritin, and realizing deep intersection of accelerators in multiple fields, was recently selected into the "Top Ten Progress of China's Surface Technology in 2026" by the Surface Engineering Branch of the Chinese Mechanical Engineering Society. According to the research team, niobium tritin can increase the operating temperature of superconducting cavities, help accelerators get rid of dependence on liquid helium, and revolutionize the operating mode of superconducting linear accelerators. It is a cutting-edge research hotspot in the field of radio frequency superconductivity. This technological achievement belongs to the deep intersection of accelerator physics, superconducting materials and surface engineering. The research team carried out systematic research through key scientific and engineering problems, revealed the relevant internal mechanisms, broke through a series of key processes, and developed the world's first liquid-free helium niobium three-tin thin film radio frequency superconducting electron accelerator, and achieved stable beam loading, thus building a systematic technical system from basic mechanism, process equipment to prototype beam testing.

5. 中国牵头制定的光栅扫描激光显示国际标准发布
The international standard for raster scanning laser displays led by China is released

中新社北京11月10日电 中国国家市场监督管理总局10日公布，中国牵头制定的光栅扫描激光显示国际标准近日由国际电工委员会(IEC)正式发布。该标准由中国专家担任项目负责人，联合比利时、美国、日本等国专家共同制定。光栅扫描是激光显示的主要技术路线之一，常见产品有微型激光投影机、AR/VR显示器(增强现实显示器和虚拟现实显示器)等。光栅扫描就像用一支极细的“激光画笔”在屏幕上画画：通过一只快速摆动的小镜子，控制激光束上下左右移动，从而扫出完整画面。

ENChina News Service, Beijing, November 10th China's State Administration for Market Regulation announced on the 10th that the international standard for raster scanning laser displays led by China was officially released by the International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC). The standard is jointly developed by Chinese experts as project leaders and experts from Belgium, the United States, Japan and other countries. Raster scanning is one of the main technical routes for laser display. Common products include micro laser projectors, AR/VR displays (augmented reality displays and virtual reality displays), etc. Raster scanning is like using a very thin "laser paintbrush" to draw on the screen: through a small mirror that swings quickly, the laser beam is controlled to move up, down, left, and right to scan out the complete picture.

6. 国际最新研究：预计2026年全球二氧化碳排放量将暂时性下降0.4%
The latest international research: Global carbon dioxide emissions are expected to temporarily decrease by 0.4% in 2026

中新网北京11月10日电 (记者 孙自法)施普林格·自然旗下专业学术期刊《自然-气候变化》11月10日在线发表一篇论文称，一项研究显示，在全球化石燃料二氧化碳排放量于2019年至2025年间以年均0.8%的速度增长后，预计2026年将暂时下降，降幅预计为0.4%。论文作者将这一下降归因于美伊霍尔木兹海峡战争导致的油价上涨，并得出结论认为，这很可能是暂时的下降，而非排放量已达到持久峰值的证据。该论文介绍，尽管面临全球能源市场动荡、政府政策重点调整、高昂的资本成本、贸易壁垒以及AI和数据中心需求不断增长等因素，向清洁能源的转型仍在继续。可再生能源和电动汽车对全球排放的抑制作用日益显著，但其效果正被不断增长的能源需求所抵消，尤其是在新兴经济体中。

ENChina News Service, Beijing, November 10 (Reporter Sun Zifa) Springer Nature's professional academic journal "Nature - Climate Change" published a paper online on November 10, saying that a study showed that after global fossil fuel carbon dioxide emissions increased at an average annual rate of 0.8% from 2019 to 2025, they are expected to temporarily decline in 2026, with the decline expected to be 0.4%. The paper's authors attributed the drop to rising oil prices as a result of the U.S.-Iran war in the Strait of Hormuz, concluding that it was likely a temporary drop rather than evidence that emissions had reached a lasting peak. According to the paper, despite factors such as global energy market turbulence, government policy priorities, high capital costs, trade barriers, and growing demand for AI and data centers, the transition to clean energy continues. Renewable energy and electric vehicles are increasingly curbing global emissions, but their effects are being offset by growing energy demand, especially in emerging economies.

8. 最新气候变化研究：全球区域性变暖或已与升温1.5 °C的世界相仿
Latest climate change research: Global regional warming may already be similar to a world warming by 1.5 °C

中新网北京11月10日电 (记者 孙自法)施普林格·自然旗下专业学术期刊《自然-气候变化》最新发表一篇论文称，一项研究结果表明，世界各地的一些地区可能已经经历了全球平均气温较工业化前水平升高1.5°C时预期升温幅度的约90%–94%，以及升温2°C时预期升温幅度的65%–76%。这项由加拿大维多利亚大学和中国南京信息工程大学学者合作完成的研究成果表明，2025年的区域升温水平与升温1.5°C的世界预期中的水平已无明显统计差异，凸显了所有地区都亟需采取适应性措施。该论文介绍，《巴黎协定》旨在将全球平均气温升幅控制在远低于较工业化前水平高2°C的水平，并努力将升温限制在1.5°C以内。然而，这些全球限制转化到不同地区时，对应升温幅度有所差异。

ENChina News Service, Beijing, November 10 (Reporter Sun Zifa) A new paper published in Nature Climate Change, a professional academic journal of Springer Nature, states that a study shows that some areas around the world may have experienced about 90%–94% of the expected warming rate when the global average temperature rises by 1.5°C above pre-industrial levels, and 65%–76% of the expected warming rate when the global average temperature rises by 2°C. The research results, a collaboration between scholars at the University of Victoria in Canada and Nanjing University of Information Science and Technology in China, show that there is no significant statistical difference between regional warming levels in 2025 and levels expected in a world with a warming of 1.5°C, highlighting the urgent need for adaptation measures in all regions. According to the paper, the Paris Agreement aims to limit the increase in global average temperature to well below 2°C above pre-industrial levels and strive to limit warming to 1.5°C. However, these global limits translate into different levels of warming in different regions.
