According to reports, Putin stated at the ceremony that the Perm is the fifth Yasen-M-class nuclear submarine to be commissioned by the Russian Navy in the past six years. 6. 多国商界人士和投资者汇聚金边探索柬埔寨合作发展机遇
Businesspeople and investors from multiple countries gather in Phnom Penh to explore opportunities for cooperative development in Cambodia

中新社金边11月27日电 (记者 杨强)2026年柬埔寨国际商务与投资论坛当地时间27日在金边举行，柬埔寨首相洪玛奈出席开幕式。来自中国、马来西亚、新加坡、日本、韩国以及欧盟等国家和地区逾千名商界人士及投资者，围绕“柬埔寨的发展，全球的机遇”等相关主题展开对话。洪玛奈在致辞中重申柬政府持续改善投资环境、开放市场以吸引更多国内外投资的承诺。他表示，在新冠疫情暴发前的20年间，柬埔寨经济持续实现高速增长，已从计划经济转型为市场经济，产业结构也由单一的传统农业向多元化发展，成为能够承接新兴和高附加值产业的经济体。

ENPhnom Penh, November 27 (Xinhua) – The 2026 Cambodia International Business and Investment Forum was held in Phnom Penh on November 27 local time, with Cambodian Prime Minister Hun Manet attending the opening ceremony. Over a thousand businesspeople and investors from China, Malaysia, Singapore, Japan, South Korea, the European Union, and other countries and regions engaged in discussions centered around themes such as "Cambodia's Development, Global Opportunities." In his address, Hun Manet reaffirmed the Cambodian government's commitment to continuously improving the investment environment and opening up the market to attract more domestic and foreign investment. He stated that in the 20 years leading up to the COVID-19 pandemic, Cambodia's economy had experienced sustained rapid growth, transitioning from a planned economy to a market economy. Its industrial structure had also evolved from a single traditional agricultural base to a diversified one, making it an economy capable of accommodating emerging and high-value-added industries.

7. 菲律宾副总统莎拉称绝不会因弹劾审判辞职
Philippine Vice President Sara Duterte says she will never resign due to an impeachment trial

中新社马尼拉11月27日电 (记者 周璟)菲律宾副总统莎拉·杜特尔特27日表示，即使面临弹劾审判，她也绝不会主动辞去副总统职务。莎拉当天在一场年终答谢午宴上回应有关要求其辞职的呼声时说，辞职“绝无可能”。她表示，主动辞职将辜负3220万名投票支持她的选民以及广大公众。菲律宾参议院弹劾法庭将于12月1日复庭，继续审理莎拉弹劾案。

ENManila, November 27 (Xinhua) – Philippine Vice President Sara Duterte stated on November 27 that she would never voluntarily resign from her position as Vice President, even if she were to face an impeachment trial. At a year-end thank-you luncheon that day, Sara responded to calls for her resignation, stating that resignation was "absolutely out of the question." She stated that voluntarily resigning would betray the 32.2 million voters who supported her and the general public. The Philippine Senate Impeachment Tribunal will reconvene on December 1 to continue hearing Sara's impeachment case.

8. 法国第三季度经济环比下降0.2%
France's economy contracted by 0.2% quarter-on-quarter in the third quarter

中新社巴黎11月27日电 (记者 李洋)法国官方机构当地时间27日发布的经济数据显示，法国经济表现低迷，今年第三季度经济环比下降0.2%。根据法国国家统计与经济研究所当天公布的经济统计，法国第三季度国内生产总值(GDP)环比下降0.2%，与此前公布的初步数据相比下调0.1个百分点。法国经济今年前三个季度的环比统计均未能录得正增长，全年经济表现难言乐观。相关统计数据显示，法国第三季度经济萎缩主要归因于投资下滑及出口下降。

ENParis, November 27th (Xinhua News Agency) – According to economic data released by French official agencies on November 27th local time, the French economy has been sluggish, with a quarter-on-quarter decline of 0.2% in the third quarter of this year. According to the economic statistics released by the French National Institute of Statistics and Economic Studies on the same day, France's gross domestic product (GDP) declined by 0.2% quarter-on-quarter in the third quarter, a downward revision of 0.1 percentage point compared to the previously released preliminary data. The French economy failed to record positive quarter-on-quarter growth in the first three quarters of this year, and its economic performance for the full year is far from optimistic. According to relevant statistical data, France's economic contraction in the third quarter was primarily attributed to declines in investment and exports.

9. 日本文部科学省系统遭非法访问 或致超16万条信息泄露
The systems of Japan's Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology were illegally accessed, potentially leading to the leakage of over 160,000 pieces of information.

中新网11月28日电 据日本共同社27日报道，日本文部科学省近日宣布，已检测到该省用于公布招标及合同信息的系统遭到非法访问，或导致超16万条信息泄露。据报道，文部科学省指出，最多可能有163953条具备投标资格的企业名称等信息，以及最多1778个该省职员等人员电子邮箱地址外泄。据报道，目前尚未发现信息被滥用的情况。

ENChina News Service, November 28 – According to a report by Japan's Kyodo News on November 27, Japan's Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology recently announced that it had detected unauthorized access to the ministry's system used for publishing tender and contract information, potentially resulting in the leakage of over 160,000 pieces of information. According to reports, the Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology stated that up to 163,953 pieces of information, including the names of companies eligible to bid, and up to 1,778 email addresses of ministry staff and other personnel may have been leaked. According to reports, there has been no evidence of the information being misused so far.

10. 泰国文促会携手高校开展教育领域合作
Thai Cultural Promotion Association joins hands with universities to carry out cooperation in education

中新网曼谷11月27日电 (王茜 梁峻祥 李映民)泰国正大管理学院27日与泰国中华文化促进会签约，该校授权泰国中华文化促进会在中国开展招生工作。根据合作安排，该协会可联动中国各地相关协会，在不同地区为正大管理学院开展招生。除招生合作外，双方还将在人员交流、联合研究、会议举办、文化交流、实习项目及其他学术活动等领域开展合作。双方希望通过此次合作，进一步加强教育、学术及文化交流。

ENChina News Service, Bangkok, November 27 (Wang Qi, Liang Junxiang, Li Yingmin) Thailand's Zhengda Management College signed a contract with the Thai Chinese Culture Promotion Association on the 27th. The school authorized the Thai Chinese Culture Promotion Association to carry out enrollment in China. According to the cooperation arrangement, the association can link up with relevant associations across China to carry out enrollment for Zhengda Management School in different regions. In addition to cooperation in enrollment, the two sides will also cooperate in areas such as personnel exchanges, joint research, conferences, cultural exchanges, internship projects and other academic activities. Both parties hope to further strengthen educational, academic and cultural exchanges through this cooperation.
