中新社金边11月3日电 (张堰林 杨强)柬埔寨首相洪玛奈当地时间11月3日表示，柬埔寨计划在200所具备条件的公立中小学校试行全日制教学，将每日教学时间从4小时延长至6小时，并分阶段向全国推广，以全面提升该国基础教育质量。洪玛奈当天在磅通省出席新建校舍启用仪式时表示，目前柬埔寨约有200所公立中小学校具备实施“4小时＋2小时”教学安排的条件，政府将以这些学校为试点，逐步扩大实施范围，落实从小学至高中12年制基础教育每日6小时在校学习的目标。他说，前述教学模式无需增加巨额开支和配备大量现代化设备，只需充分利用现有资源并适当延长教学时间，即可让学生正常学习。此举不仅有助于提升教育质量、培养人才，亦可有效减少青少年课余时间在街头飙车等不良行为，减轻家庭与社会负担。

ENChina News Service, Phnom Penh, November 3 (Zhang Yanlin Yang Qiang) Cambodian Prime Minister Hong Manai said on November 3, local time, that Cambodia plans to trial full-time teaching in 200 qualified public primary and secondary schools, extending the daily teaching time from 4 hours to 6 hours, and promote it nationwide in stages to comprehensively improve the quality of basic education in the country. When attending the opening ceremony of the new school building in Kampong Thom Province that day, Hong Manai said that currently about 200 public primary and secondary schools in Cambodia have the conditions to implement the "4 hours + 2 hours" teaching arrangement. The government will use these schools as pilots to gradually expand the scope of implementation and implement the goal of 6 hours of in-school learning per day for 12-year basic education from primary school to high school. He said that the aforementioned teaching model does not require huge expenditures and a large number of modern equipment. It only needs to make full use of existing resources and appropriately extend the teaching time to allow students to learn normally. This move will not only help improve the quality of education and cultivate talents, but also effectively reduce the negative behaviors of teenagers such as racing on the streets in their spare time, reducing the burden on families and society.

10. 菲律宾央行：货币政策仍处紧缩周期 通胀预计数月后见顶
Philippine Central Bank: Monetary policy is still in a tightening cycle and inflation is expected to peak in a few months

中新社马尼拉11月4日电 (记者 周璟)菲律宾央行行长雷莫洛纳3日表示，鉴于通胀仍处高位，菲律宾央行目前处于货币紧缩周期，预计通胀将在未来数月达到峰值。雷莫洛纳当天接受采访时说，通胀率不会迅速回落，央行将继续密切关注物价走势，并综合考虑通胀水平和经济增长情况制定货币政策。目前尚无法确定紧缩周期是否会延续至2027年。菲律宾9月整体通胀率升至7.2%，为近五个月最高；核心通胀率升至4.7%，创近三年来新高。

ENChina News Service, Manila, November 4 (Reporter Zhou Jing) Philippine Central Bank Governor Ray Molona said on the 3rd that given that inflation is still high, the Philippine central bank is currently in a monetary tightening cycle and expects inflation to peak in the next few months. Remolona said in an interview that day that the inflation rate will not drop quickly, and the central bank will continue to pay close attention to price trends and formulate monetary policies taking into account inflation levels and economic growth. It is unclear whether the tightening cycle will extend into 2027. The Philippines' overall inflation rate rose to 7.2% in September, the highest in the past five months; the core inflation rate rose to 4.7%, a new high in the past three years.
