中新网雅加达11月21日电 (记者 李志全)心联通·文互鉴——“讲中国故事”暨印尼摄影家赴福建采风成果展20日在雅加达举行。中国驻印尼使馆文化参赞王思平表示，本次活动以“心联通·文互鉴”为主题，既是一场“讲中国故事”的文化分享，也是一次以影像为媒、以文化会友的交流盛会。希望通过主题讲座、青年交流、文艺展演和文化体验等多种形式，让更多印尼朋友特别是青年一代，从身边的故事和真实的视角认识中国、了解中国。印尼福建社团联谊总会总主席俞雨龄认为，摄影是跨越语言的艺术，文化是连接心灵的桥梁。

ENChina News Service, Jakarta, November 21 (Reporter Li Zhiquan) Heart-to-heart communication and cultural mutual learning - "Telling Chinese Stories" and an exhibition of the results of Indonesian photographers' collection in Fujian were held in Jakarta on the 20th. Wang Siping, Cultural Counselor of the Chinese Embassy in Indonesia, said that this event, with the theme of "Connecting Hearts and Mutual Learning through Culture", is not only a cultural sharing of "telling Chinese stories", but also an exchange event using images as a medium and cultural friends. It is hoped that through various forms such as theme lectures, youth exchanges, artistic performances and cultural experiences, more Indonesian friends, especially the younger generation, will know and understand China from the stories around them and from a real perspective. Yu Yuling, general chairman of the Indonesian Fujian Federation of Associations, believes that photography is an art that transcends languages, and culture is a bridge connecting souls.

9. 日本核心CPI连续62个月上涨 民调显示高市应对通胀措施遭质疑
Japan's core CPI has risen for 62 consecutive months, polls show that the market's measures to deal with inflation are being questioned

中新网11月20日电 日本总务省20日公布的数据显示，剔除生鲜食品后，日本10月核心消费价格指数(CPI)同比上涨2.2%，涨幅连续两个月超过日本央行设定的2%目标水平。日媒指出，截至10月，日本核心CPI已连续62个月同比上涨。数据显示，能源价格上涨是推动日本物价上涨的重要因素。受政府削减电力和城市燃气费用补贴，以及中东局势紧张推高原油价格等因素影响，日本能源价格攀升。

ENChina News Service, November 20. Data released by Japan's Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications on the 20th showed that after excluding fresh food, Japan's core consumer price index (CPI) rose by 2.2% year-on-year in October, exceeding the 2% target level set by the Bank of Japan for two consecutive months. Japanese media pointed out that as of October, Japan’s core CPI had risen year-on-year for 62 consecutive months. Data show that rising energy prices are an important factor driving up price increases in Japan. Japan's energy prices have risen due to factors such as government cuts in subsidies for electricity and city gas bills, as well as tensions in the Middle East pushing up crude oil prices.

10. 克里米亚大桥遭袭 普京发声
Crimean bridge attacked, Putin speaks out

中新网11月20日电 综合俄媒报道，俄罗斯总统普京19日表示，俄方此前曾评估与有关方面就海上停火及停止攻击能源设施举行谈判的可能性，但克里米亚大桥再次遭袭后，俄方不会就此进行谈判。据俄罗斯总统网站消息，在回应关于克里米亚大桥近日多次遭袭及受损情况的提问时，普京回应称，克里米亚大桥是民用设施，俄方从未通过该桥运输军事装备和弹药，也没有通过该桥运输石油及石油制品。普京还表示，俄方将对袭击作出回应，且力度将比乌方对克里米亚大桥的攻击更大。据俄媒报道，克里米亚大桥16日至18日连续三天遭到乌军袭击，致使大桥交通受到影响。

ENChina News Service, November 20 (Xinhua) According to comprehensive Russian media reports, Russian President Vladimir Putin said on the 19th that Russia had previously evaluated the possibility of negotiating a maritime ceasefire and stopping attacks on energy facilities with relevant parties, but after the Crimean Bridge was attacked again, Russia would not negotiate on this. According to the Russian presidential website, in response to questions about the recent attacks and damage to the Crimean Bridge, Putin responded that the Crimean Bridge is a civilian facility and Russia has never transported military equipment and ammunition through the bridge, nor has it transported oil and petroleum products through the bridge. Putin also said that Russia would respond to the attack with greater intensity than Ukraine's attack on the Crimean bridge. According to Russian media reports, the Crimean Bridge was attacked by the Ukrainian army for three consecutive days from the 16th to the 18th, causing traffic on the bridge to be affected.
