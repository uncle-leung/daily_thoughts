7. 国际最新研究：非洲水资源并未得到充分利用
Latest international research: Africa’s water resources are not fully utilized

中新网北京10月29日电 (记者 孙自法)国际学术期刊《自然》最新发表一篇环境科学论文指出，一项研究结果表明，非洲部分地区的用水紧张可能源于基础设施不到位，而不是缺少水源。这也说明，非洲的水资源并未得到充分利用。该研究显示，非洲的用水需求在2010年到2020年期间增长了约26%，但2020年仍有约7.17亿人用不上自来水。论文作者绘制出不同领域的用水需求全景图，指出增加非洲水资源可利用量所需解决的问题，而增加水资源可利用量也是可持续发展目标6“清洁饮水和卫生设施”(SDG6)的内容之一。

ENChina News Service, Beijing, October 29 (Reporter Sun Zifa) A recent environmental science paper published in the international academic journal "Nature" pointed out that a research result shows that water stress in some parts of Africa may be caused by inadequate infrastructure rather than a lack of water sources. This also shows that Africa’s water resources have not been fully utilized. The study shows that water demand in Africa increased by about 26% between 2010 and 2020, but about 717 million people still did not have access to tap water in 2020. The authors of the paper draw a panoramic view of water demand in different fields and point out the problems that need to be solved to increase the availability of water resources in Africa. Increasing the availability of water resources is also one of the contents of Sustainable Development Goal 6 "Clean Water and Sanitation" (SDG6).
