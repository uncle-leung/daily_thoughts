According to reports, this plan is closely aligned with the autonomous region's "15th Five-Year Plan" development plan and land and space planning, and highlights four major directions: deepening the "one map" application of land and space, improving differentiated and refined use control systems, and promoting refined space governance; establishing a full-chain mechanism for inventory revitalization, inefficient transformation, and incremental improvement to promote green utilization of resources; building a digital base for spatio-temporal information to create a full-coverage, full-time, and full-chain smart supervision system to achieve intelligent management upgrades. 中新网11月12日电 据国家铁路局微信公众号消息，国家铁路局近日正式批准发布我国铁路行业首个新能源机车整车级行业技术标准，成功填补了我国新能源机车领域行业标准空白，为铁路装备绿色转型提供了坚实的技术支撑。作为新能源机车车辆领域重要技术文件，该标准内容覆盖新能源机车设计开发、试验验证、生产制造和运用维护全过程，系统提出新能源机车运用条件、分类命名、机车总体、关键系统、检查试验等全要素规定，实现了由干线铁路向更多铁路场景拓展，全面满足钢铁、冶金、矿山、电力、煤炭、港口等企业多场景铁路运输需要。该标准聚焦新能源机车研发、验证、应用全寿命周期共性需求，按课题先行、试点验证、标准成型“三步走”扎实推进，统筹开展了新能源动力系统技术要求、能效评价、能耗及续航能力测定等专题研究，一体化破解范围界定、安全管控、效能评价、技术引领等重点问题，推动前瞻布局和落地实施有机融合。

ENChina News Service, November 12. According to the WeChat public account of the National Railway Administration, the National Railway Administration recently officially approved the release of China's railway industry's first complete vehicle-level industry technical standard for new energy locomotives, successfully filling the gap in industry standards in the field of new energy locomotives in China and providing solid technical support for the green transformation of railway equipment. As an important technical document in the field of new energy rolling stock, the standard covers the entire process of new energy locomotive design and development, test verification, production and manufacturing, operation and maintenance, and systematically proposes all-element regulations for new energy locomotives such as operating conditions, classification and naming, locomotive overall, key systems, inspection and testing, etc., realizing the expansion from mainline railways to more railway scenarios, and fully meeting the multi-scenario railway transportation needs of enterprises such as steel, metallurgy, mining, electric power, coal, ports, etc. The standard focuses on the common needs of the entire life cycle of new energy locomotive R&D, verification, and application. It is solidly advanced in a "three-step" process of project first, pilot verification, and standard formation. It coordinates and carries out special research on new energy power system technical requirements, energy efficiency evaluation, energy consumption and endurance measurement, and integrates key issues such as scope definition, safety management and control, performance evaluation, and technology leadership to promote the organic integration of forward-looking layout and implementation.
