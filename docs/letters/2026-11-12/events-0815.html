4. 中国发射探源计划探路者卫星
China launches Pathfinder satellite

中新社北京11月11日电 (记者 马帅莎)据中国航天科技集团消息，北京时间11月11日19时41分，中国在酒泉卫星发射中心使用长征二号丁运载火箭，将探源计划探路者卫星发射升空，卫星顺利进入预定轨道，发射任务取得圆满成功。长征二号丁运载火箭是一款常温液体二级运载火箭，具有可靠性高、经济性好、适应性强等特点，可支持单星、多星并联、串联、搭载等多种形式的发射需求，其700公里太阳同步轨道运载能力为1.3吨。此次任务是长征系列运载火箭的第677次飞行。

ENChina News Service, Beijing, November 11 (Reporter Ma Shuaisha) According to China Aerospace Science and Technology Corporation, at 19:41 on November 11, Beijing time, China used the Long March 2D carrier rocket at the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center to launch the Pathfinder satellite of the Origin Discovery Program. The satellite successfully entered the predetermined orbit, and the launch mission was a complete success. The Long March-2D launch vehicle is a room-temperature liquid two-stage launch vehicle with the characteristics of high reliability, good economy, and strong adaptability. It can support various launch requirements such as single satellite, multi-satellite parallel, series, and piggyback. Its 700-kilometer sun-synchronous orbit has a carrying capacity of 1.3 tons. This mission is the 677th flight of the Long March series of launch vehicles.
