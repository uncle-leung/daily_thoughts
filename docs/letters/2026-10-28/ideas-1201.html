1. 三项科学证明先等外部核完
The three scientific proofs must wait for external verification to be completed.

半岛电视台10月27日报道，OpenAI周一称其模型在调和分析、复杂度理论和量子场论做出三项发现，包括首次在所有空间维度证明局部平滑猜想，并称推翻了指数时间假说。麻省理工学院的Ryan Williams告诉该报，这一假说自1990年代末被广泛相信，大量理论计算机科学建立在它为真的前提上，他们正在核验证明是否成立。同一篇报道还写道，物理部分暂缓公开，合作专家要先写出自己的解读。今天先把公司宣布和同行核验分开，未核完之前，它仍是一项待查主张。

ENAl Jazeera reported on October 27 that OpenAI said on Monday that its model had made three discoveries in harmonic analysis, complexity theory and quantum field theory, including proving the local smoothness conjecture in all spatial dimensions for the first time, and claiming that it had overturned the exponential time hypothesis. Ryan Williams of the Massachusetts Institute of Technology told the newspaper that this hypothesis has been widely believed since the late 1990s, and a large amount of theoretical computer science is based on the premise that it is true, and they are verifying whether it is true. The same report also wrote that the physics part has been postponed to the public, and the cooperating experts must first write their own interpretations. Today, let’s separate the company’s announcement and peer verification. Before the verification is completed, it is still a claim to be investigated.

2. 能改的世界，两分钟后会漂

ENTechCrunch reported on October 27 that Google DeepMind released the world model Genie 4 as a research preview. Users can use text to change the weather, camera position, or add characters during the simulation. The model will simultaneously generate music, environmental sounds, and dialogue, with a continuous scene of up to about two minutes. According to the report, Gemini is responsible for the disassembly steps and checking the screen. The team also admitted that once the simulation is extended, objects and details will still drift. Whether the audio is accurate and whether the text on the screen can be recognized has not yet been resolved. It is currently only available to trusted testers and will be available to Google AI Ultra subscribers and select interface partners in a few weeks. If you want to hand over a generated world to others as a basis today, first ask if the original object is still there after two minutes.

3. 专家以为判断是自己做的
Experts think they make their own judgments

Anthropic 10月27日发表研究报告称，50名有因果推断经验的分析师使用Claude Code完成一项农业合作社的统计任务时，四个分析选择里有86%顺着了智能体事先被设定、但未告知分析师的倾向。事后77%的人至少复述过一次智能体的论证，只有15%注明来源，而跟得紧的人并不觉得最终建议更不像自己的。研究写明，这种倾向是实验故意加强的，不能直接当成日常办公的普遍比例。今天把判断交给智能体之前，先写下自己原本要选的那一项，回头再看有没有被带过去。

ENAnthropic published a research report on October 27 stating that when 50 analysts with causal inference experience used Claude Code to complete a statistical task on an agricultural cooperative, 86% of the four analysis choices followed the tendency of the agent that was preset but not informed to the analysts. Afterward, 77% of people repeated the agent's argument at least once, and only 15% cited the source. Those who followed closely did not feel that the final suggestion was even less their own. The study shows that this tendency was deliberately strengthened by the experiment and cannot be directly regarded as a general proportion of daily office work. Before handing over the judgment to the intelligent agent today, write down the item you originally wanted to choose, and look back to see if it was taken over.

ENFrance 24 quoted AFP on October 27. Celeste Saulo, head of the World Meteorological Organization, said that the intensity of this round of El Niño may exceed the monitoring charts used in the past four decades, and called for early action together with the United Nations humanitarian chief Tom Fletcher; Fletcher warned that 50 million people may face more severe hunger. El Niño-related flooding in northeastern Argentina has killed two people in recent days, and in French Guiana, shipping has been gradually ground to a halt as river levels plummeted, with authorities setting up air bridges to provide air supplies to some 40,000 residents who have no access to roads. If today's itinerary, warehousing or cross-border fulfillment is tied to the weather in a certain area, first check the rainfall and high temperatures in the next few weeks. What El Niño changes is that certain types of weather are more likely to occur in a season, and each specific disaster cannot be attributed to it alone.

All reports are submitted, but there is still a gap in late diagnosis

世界卫生组织欧洲区域办事处代理负责人Corinne Capuano在10月27日的公开讲话中说，今年1月至10月，办事处分析了4.8万条潜在卫生威胁信息，其中35条需要采取行动，区域内每个缔约国也首次都提交了《国际卫生条例》年度报告。她同时指出，五分之一的结核病病例未被诊断或报告，半数艾滋病毒诊断来得太晚，75岁以下仍有180万人的死亡本可通过预防或治疗避免。今天读这类通报，先看发现得晚不晚，再看总数大不大。报告交齐只说明监测在运转，漏诊和晚诊仍会把负担留到后面。

ENCorinne Capuano, acting head of the World Health Organization's European Regional Office, said in a public speech on October 27 that from January to October this year, the office analyzed 48,000 pieces of potential health threat information, 35 of which required action. For the first time, every state party in the region submitted an annual report on the International Health Regulations. She also pointed out that one-fifth of tuberculosis cases are not diagnosed or reported, half of HIV diagnoses come too late, and there are still 1.8 million deaths under the age of 75 that could have been avoided through prevention or treatment. When reading this kind of bulletin today, first look at whether it was discovered too late, and then look at whether the total number is large. Submission of all reports only shows that surveillance is functioning, while missed diagnosis and late diagnosis will still leave the burden behind.

9. 供应商入股，需求要分开看
When suppliers invest in shares, demand must be looked at separately.

CNBC 10月27日报道，英伟达和AMD分别宣布认购Meta股权发行中的30亿美元和10亿美元；Meta周一称，将先通过承销公开发行筹资350亿美元，再以按市价陆续出售的方式另筹至多600亿美元，合计约950亿美元，用于人工智能基础设施。英伟达还称，Meta将扩大Vera Rubin图形处理器和Vera中央处理器的部署，并加深双方在智能体Muse上的合作。今天看这种公告，先把客户在买芯片和芯片商在买客户的股份分开。供应商入股大客户时，订单和投资会叠在同一组数字里，需求看起来会比实际使用更满。

ENCNBC reported on October 27 that Nvidia and AMD respectively announced the subscription of US$3 billion and US$1 billion in Meta's equity issuance; Meta said on Monday that it would first raise US$35 billion through an underwritten public offering, and then raise up to another US$60 billion through successive sales at market prices, totaling approximately US$95 billion, for artificial intelligence infrastructure. Nvidia also said that Meta will expand the deployment of Vera Rubin graphics processors and Vera central processors, and deepen the cooperation between the two parties on the intelligent Muse. When looking at this kind of announcement today, first separate the customers’ purchases of chips and the chip manufacturers’ purchases of customers’ shares. When a supplier acquires a stake in a large customer, orders and investments will be stacked in the same set of numbers, and demand will appear to be greater than actual usage.
