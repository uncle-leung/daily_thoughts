1. 前9个月规上工业企业利润同比增长15.5%
In the first nine months, profits of industrial enterprises increased by 15.5% year-on-year.

国家统计局27日公布的数据显示，1—9月份，全国规模以上工业企业实现利润总额60337.4亿元，同比增长15.5%，今年以来持续保持两位数增长。1—9月份，电子行业利润同比增长1倍，拉动全部规模以上工业企业利润增长9.6个百分点。同期，规模以上高技术制造业利润同比增长52.0%，增速高于全部规模以上工业36.5个百分点。1—9月份，规模以上工业企业营业收入利润率为5.68%，同比提高0.44个百分点。

ENData released by the National Bureau of Statistics on the 27th showed that from January to September, industrial enterprises above designated size across the country achieved a total profit of 6.03374 billion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 15.5%, and have continued to maintain double-digit growth this year. From January to September, the profits of the electronics industry doubled year-on-year, driving the profits of all industrial enterprises above designated size to increase by 9.6 percentage points. During the same period, the profits of high-tech manufacturing industries above designated size increased by 52.0% year-on-year, and the growth rate was 36.5 percentage points higher than that of all industries above designated size. From January to September, the operating income profit margin of industrial enterprises above designated size was 5.68%, a year-on-year increase of 0.44 percentage points.

The regulations also require the establishment of a free area on the homepage, focusing on recommending high-quality free content, streamlining the number of paid columns, and clearly displaying program charging information. 6. 能源保供中长期合同管理办法12月1日起施行
Measures for the Management of Medium and Long-term Energy Supply Guarantee Contracts will come into effect on December 1

国家发展改革委近日印发《能源保供中长期合同管理办法（试行）》，自2026年12月1日起施行。办法共6章45条，对电煤、电力、天然气保供中长期合同的签订、履行、监管等活动作出规定。办法明确，能源企业负有保障安全稳定供应的社会责任，供需各方应依法依规签订并履行合同。办法提出原则上优先保障民生用能需求，在确保安全稳定供应的前提下，供需双方协商一致后可以变更调整合同。

ENThe National Development and Reform Commission recently issued the "Measures for the Administration of Medium and Long-term Contracts for Guaranteed Energy Supply (Trial)", which will be effective from December 1, 2026. The measures include 6 chapters and 45 articles, stipulating the signing, performance, supervision and other activities of medium and long-term contracts for the guaranteed supply of thermal coal, electricity and natural gas. The measures clearly state that energy companies have the social responsibility to ensure safe and stable supply, and all parties involved in supply and demand should sign and perform contracts in accordance with laws and regulations. The method proposes that in principle, priority should be given to ensuring energy demand for people's livelihood. On the premise of ensuring safe and stable supply, the contract can be changed and adjusted after both parties reach consensus.

日本政府27日批准政令，列明东京以外行政区成为指定“副首都”需满足的条件。政令要求相关道府县经济规模占日本国内生产总值2.1%以上，辖区内至少有一座城市人口占全国人口1.2%以上，位置可避开东京大地震或富士山爆发等灾害影响，并按规定重新构建行政体制。相关法案定于本月30日施行。按照已公布标准，北海道、爱知县、大阪府、福冈县都可能具备资格，四地都计划争取。

ENThe Japanese government approved a decree on the 27th, setting out the conditions that administrative regions outside Tokyo need to meet to become designated "vice capitals". The government order requires that the economic scale of the relevant prefectures and counties account for more than 2.1% of Japan's GDP, that the population of at least one city within the jurisdiction accounts for more than 1.2% of the national population, that the location can avoid the impact of disasters such as the Tokyo Earthquake or the eruption of Mount Fuji, and that the administrative system be restructured in accordance with regulations. The relevant bill is scheduled to come into effect on the 30th of this month. According to the published standards, Hokkaido, Aichi Prefecture, Osaka Prefecture, and Fukuoka Prefecture may all be eligible, and all four places plan to compete.

9. 天然气出口国论坛峰会发表莫斯科宣言
Gas Exporting Countries Forum Summit Issues Moscow Declaration

天然气出口国论坛第八次峰会27日在莫斯科国际贸易中心举行，当天闭幕并发表《莫斯科宣言》。宣言重申天然气出口国对本国自然资源拥有绝对、不可动摇的主权权利。宣言指出，成员国对地缘政治局势给全球天然气市场稳定、安全和可持续性造成的不利影响深表关切，坚决反对未经联合国批准的单边制裁措施。宣言还呼吁推动天然气贸易和物流路线多样化，发展区域天然气枢纽和过境基础设施。

ENThe eighth summit of the Natural Gas Exporting Countries Forum was held at the Moscow International Trade Center on the 27th. It concluded that day and issued the "Moscow Declaration". The Declaration reaffirms that natural gas exporting countries have absolute and unshakable sovereign rights over their natural resources. The declaration stated that member states are deeply concerned about the adverse impact of the geopolitical situation on the stability, security and sustainability of the global natural gas market, and firmly oppose unilateral sanctions without United Nations approval. The declaration also calls for promoting the diversification of gas trade and logistics routes and the development of regional gas hubs and transit infrastructure.

10. 刚果（金）本轮埃博拉确诊升至10060例
The number of confirmed cases of Ebola in the current round of Ebola in the Democratic Republic of the Congo rises to 10,060

刚果（金）卫生部27日发布的疫情报告显示，截至25日，该国本轮埃博拉疫情累计确诊10060例，其中死亡4887例。该国今年5月宣布疫情暴发，疫情已波及全国26个省中的8个省。东部伊图里省累计确诊病例占全国总数的70%以上。23日，一项针对本迪布焦型埃博拉病毒候选疫苗的临床试验在伊图里省首府布尼亚完成首名受试者接种。

ENThe epidemic report released by the Ministry of Health of the Democratic Republic of the Congo on the 27th showed that as of the 25th, a total of 10,060 cases of this Ebola epidemic in the country had been confirmed, including 4,887 deaths. The country declared an outbreak in May this year, and the epidemic has affected 8 of the 26 provinces in the country. The cumulative confirmed cases in the eastern province of Ituri account for more than 70% of the country's total. On the 23rd, a clinical trial of a candidate vaccine for the Bundibuggio Ebola virus completed the vaccination of the first subject in Bunia, the capital of Ituri Province.
