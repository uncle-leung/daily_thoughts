10月30日，在习近平主席和瑞士联邦主席帕姆兰的共同见证下，商务部部长王文涛与瑞士联邦经济、教研部国务秘书兼经济总局局长布德里格在京签署中瑞自贸协定升级议定书。根据升级议定书，中瑞双方将在原自贸协定基础上，共同作出货物、服务、投资高水平开放承诺，并进一步升级原产地、贸易便利化、技术性贸易壁垒、数字贸易、竞争、贸易与可持续发展等领域规则。中瑞自贸协定的成功升级，将进一步深化中瑞经贸合作，为双边贸易投资自由化便利化提供更坚实的制度保障，展现了双方在当前充满不确定性的国际环境下，支持自由贸易、开放合作和基于规则的多边贸易体制的鲜明立场。中瑞两国于2013年签署自贸协定，2014年7月协定生效实施。

ENOn October 30, under the joint witness of President Xi Jinping and President of the Swiss Confederation Parmland, Minister of Commerce Wang Wentao and Secretary of State of the Swiss Federal Ministry of Economics, Education and Research and Director of the General Administration of Economic Affairs Budrig signed the protocol for upgrading the China-Switzerland Free Trade Agreement in Beijing. According to the upgraded protocol, China and Switzerland will jointly make high-level commitments to open goods, services, and investments on the basis of the original free trade agreement, and further upgrade rules in areas such as origin, trade facilitation, technical trade barriers, digital trade, competition, trade and sustainable development. The successful upgrade of the China-Switzerland Free Trade Agreement will further deepen China-Switzerland economic and trade cooperation, provide a more solid institutional guarantee for the liberalization and facilitation of bilateral trade and investment, and demonstrate the clear stance of both parties in supporting free trade, open cooperation and the rules-based multilateral trading system in the current international environment full of uncertainties. China and Switzerland signed a free trade agreement in 2013, and the agreement came into effect in July 2014.
