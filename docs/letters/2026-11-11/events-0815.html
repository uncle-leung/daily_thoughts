Lombardelli was a member of the majority at last week's monetary policy meeting, with 8 votes in favor and 1 vote against raising interest rates to 4%. She said that there is a possibility of raising interest rates in the monetary policy meetings to be held in the near future. Each meeting will be dynamically evaluated based on the latest data. The situation in the Middle East, prices and labor market performance will all affect her voting tendency. She focused on analyzing the British labor market, saying that the market vitality is weak, and the pace of corporate recruitment and personnel turnover has slowed down simultaneously.

The project is called "Atlantic Network" and is part of the first phase of the British Army's "Atlantic Fortress" plan. This autonomous system network can be deployed in coordination with the British manned ships, relying on artificial intelligence technology to integrate underwater collection data, automatically generate sea area situation information, push early warning signals to anti-submarine warships and nuclear submarines in advance, and make up for the monitoring blind spots of traditional maritime patrols. The relevant procurement framework agreement has been signed with local British companies.

9. 泰国内阁批准数据中心七级监管政策
Thailand’s Cabinet approves seven-level regulatory policy for data centers

中新社曼谷11月10日电 (王茜 李映民)泰国内阁10日批准数据中心监管政策，决定按用电规模实施七级分类管理，20兆瓦及以上项目将面临更严格的选址要求。泰国副总理兼财政部长艾尼迪表示，政府将设立一站式服务中心，统筹数据中心项目审批。据泰国国家经济和社会发展委员会秘书长丹努差介绍，数据中心将按照用电规模划分为七级：低于0.5兆瓦、0.5至5兆瓦、5至10兆瓦、10至20兆瓦、20至50兆瓦、50至200兆瓦以及200兆瓦及以上。其中，20兆瓦以下项目可设在商业区域，20兆瓦及以上项目则须设在工业区域。

ENChina News Service, Bangkok, November 10 (Wang Qi, Li Yingmin) The Thai Cabinet approved the data center regulatory policy on the 10th and decided to implement seven-level classified management according to the scale of power consumption. Projects of 20 MW and above will face more stringent site selection requirements. Thailand's Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Finance Enidi said that the government will establish a one-stop service center to coordinate the approval of data center projects. According to Danucha, Secretary-General of Thailand’s National Economic and Social Development Council, data centers will be divided into seven levels according to the scale of power consumption: less than 0.5 MW, 0.5 to 5 MW, 5 to 10 MW, 10 to 20 MW, 20 to 50 MW, 50 to 200 MW and 200 MW and above. Among them, projects below 20 MW can be located in commercial areas, while projects of 20 MW and above must be located in industrial areas.
