The "Catalogue" establishes a "5+1" law enforcement guidance system, covering five major fields: coal mines, non-coal mines, industry and trade, fireworks and firecracker operations, and safety evaluation, testing and inspection. The hazardous chemicals field implements the national unified catalog. The "Catalog" focuses on weak links that can easily lead to major accidents, guides law enforcement forces to tilt toward major risks and major hidden dangers, further reduces the space for law enforcement discretion, promotes unified handling standards for similar illegal situations, curbs phenomena such as selective law enforcement and small penalties for major cases, and prevents and resolves major safety risks from the source.

6. 台湾10月PMI扩张放缓 专家指AI供应链缺料所致

中新社台北11月3日电 台湾10月制造业采购经理人指数(PMI)扩张放缓引发关注。对此，专家分析指出，人工智能(AI)产业持续支撑岛内景气扩张，但近期供应链缺料导致制造业增速放缓。标普全球2日公布的调查数据显示，台湾10月制造业PMI从9月的56.7降至54.9；同一天，台湾中华经济研究院(简称“中经院”)公布10月制造业PMI降至62.5，指数下跌0.9个百分点。台湾《联合报》3日报道，中经院院长连贤明分析，近期台湾AI服务器需求强劲，但存储器等关键零部件供应受限，减缓整体出货进度，也成为影响台湾制造业扩张速度的重要因素之一。

ENChina News Service, Taipei, November 3 (Xinhua) The slowdown in the expansion of Taiwan's manufacturing purchasing managers' index (PMI) in October has caused concern. In this regard, expert analysis pointed out that the artificial intelligence (AI) industry continues to support the island's economic expansion, but the recent shortage of materials in the supply chain has led to a slowdown in manufacturing growth. Survey data released by S&P Global on the 2nd showed that Taiwan's manufacturing PMI fell to 54.9 in October from 56.7 in September. On the same day, Taiwan's China Economic Research Institute (referred to as "China Economic Research Institute") announced that the manufacturing PMI in October fell to 62.5, and the index fell by 0.9 percentage points. Taiwan's "United Daily News" reported on the 3rd that Lian Xianming, president of the China Academy of Economics, analyzed that demand for Taiwan's AI servers has been strong recently, but the supply of key components such as memory has been limited, slowing down the overall shipment progress and becoming one of the important factors affecting the expansion speed of Taiwan's manufacturing industry.

3. 泰国内阁批准社会保障法修正案草案 最高参保年龄提高至65岁
Thailand’s Cabinet approves draft amendment to Social Security Law, raising maximum insurance age to 65

中新社曼谷11月3日电 (王茜 李映民)泰国总理府副发言人帕达拉斯米3日表示，泰国内阁当天批准劳动部提交的《社会保障法》修正案草案，拟将《社会保障法》第33条规定的雇员最高参保年龄由60岁提高至65岁，以适应人口老龄化及劳动市场变化。此外，参保人员离职后，子女抚养津贴拟继续发放6个月；生育停工补偿将作相应调整，使其与劳动保护法律规定相衔接。对于非因工致残人员，收入损失补偿比例拟由原来的工资最高50%提高至最高70%。在养老保障方面，累计缴纳社会保障金满180个月的参保人员，达到55岁或参保身份终止时，可选择领取按月发放的养老金或一次性养老补助。

ENChina News Service, Bangkok, November 3 (Wang Qi, Li Yingmin) Padarasmi, deputy spokesperson of the Prime Minister's Office of Thailand, said on the 3rd that the Thai Cabinet approved the draft amendment to the "Social Security Law" submitted by the Ministry of Labor on the same day, which plans to increase the maximum insurance age for employees stipulated in Article 33 of the "Social Security Law" from 60 to 65 years old to adapt to the aging of the population and changes in the labor market. In addition, child support allowances are planned to continue to be paid for 6 months after insured personnel leave their jobs; compensation for childbirth suspension will be adjusted accordingly to make it consistent with labor protection laws and regulations. For persons with disabilities not caused by work, the compensation rate for loss of income is planned to be increased from the original maximum 50% of wages to a maximum of 70%. In terms of pension security, insured persons who have paid social security contributions for 180 months in total can choose to receive monthly pensions or one-time pension subsidies when they reach the age of 55 or their insured status is terminated.
