ENChina News Service, November 3. According to the WeChat public account of Xining Wildlife Park, on October 7, 2026, the Qinghai Provincial Wildlife Rescue and Breeding Center received a severely injured snow leopard cub handed over by the Tianjun Management Branch of the Qinghai Lake Scenic Area Administration Bureau, named "Ling Xiaohan". The examination found that it suffered from multiple skull fractures accompanied by multifocal cerebral hemorrhage, cerebral edema and intracranial infection. Despite 26 days of full-scale rescue efforts, "Ling Xiaohan" unfortunately died of his injuries. After the center took "Ling Xiaohan" back, it immediately took treatment measures such as body condition correction, nutritional support, and anti-infection to save his life. During the treatment, "Ling Xiaohan" frequently experienced neurological symptoms such as prolonged and severe convulsions. The center quickly collaborated with domestic experts in veterinary imaging, neurology, and surgery for remote consultation. Computed tomography (CT) examination confirmed that he had multiple skull fractures accompanied by multifocal cerebral hemorrhage and cerebral edema. The center quickly adjusted the treatment plan and carried out continuous severe rescue work.

中新社华盛顿11月2日电 (记者 沙晗汀)中美国家公园影像展当地时间10月30日在华盛顿中国驻美国大使馆举办，展出来自中美两国的摄影师在中美多个国家公园拍摄的精美影像。此次展出的影像由美国国家地理学会组织的中美联合摄影队拍摄。过去两年中，摄影队深入中国的大熊猫国家公园和周边4处自然保护地、美国的冰川国家公园和奥林匹克国家公园等地，用影像记录两国极具特色的自然秘境，探求不同文化对自然的理解和热爱。中国驻美大使馆参赞刘畅表示，这些富有感染力的影像故事展现了中美两国的自然之美，也有助于加深两国民众的相互理解。

ENChina News Service, Washington, November 2 (Reporter Sha Hanting) The China-US National Parks Image Exhibition was held on October 30, local time, at the Chinese Embassy in Washington, DC. It displayed exquisite images taken by photographers from China and the United States in many national parks in China and the United States. The images on display were taken by a Sino-US joint photography team organized by the National Geographic Society. In the past two years, the photography team has gone deep into China's Giant Panda National Park and four surrounding nature reserves, as well as Glacier National Park and Olympic National Park in the United States. They have used images to record the unique natural secrets of the two countries and explore different cultures' understanding and love for nature. Liu Chang, counselor of the Chinese Embassy in the United States, said that these touching video stories show the natural beauty of China and the United States and help deepen mutual understanding between the people of the two countries.

6. 英国探险家历时28年徒步环球终返家乡
British explorer returns home after 28 years of walking around the world

中新社伦敦11月2日电 (记者 欧阳开宇)当地时间11月2日，英国探险家卡尔·布什比重返家乡赫尔，正式收官耗时28年的环球徒步远征，为这场超长旅途画上句号。布什比于1998年从智利启程，全程坚守零机动出行原则，仅依靠步行、涉水横渡推进行程，总里程达3.1万英里。路透社报道，旅途纵贯南美洲、北美洲、亚洲、欧洲，他徒步穿越丛林、荒漠与极地冰原，徒步踏过白令海峡冰面，并先后横渡里海、英吉利海峡，以纯人力方式完成跨洲跨国行进。现年57岁的布什比曾服役于英国空降兵团，原本计划十年完成全程，却因各类突发状况大幅延期。

ENChina News Service, London, November 2 (Reporter Ouyang Kaiyu) On November 2, local time, British explorer Carl Bushby returned to his hometown of Hull, officially ending his 28-year global walking expedition, bringing an end to this ultra-long journey. Bushby set off from Chile in 1998 and adhered to the principle of zero-motorized travel throughout the journey, relying only on walking and wading across water to advance the journey, with a total mileage of 31,000 miles. Reuters reported that the journey spanned South America, North America, Asia and Europe. He walked through jungles, deserts and polar ice sheets, walked across the ice of the Bering Strait, and crossed the Caspian Sea and the English Channel successively, completing his transcontinental and cross-border journey by pure human means. The 57-year-old Bushby once served in the British Airborne Corps. He originally planned to complete the entire process in ten years, but was significantly delayed due to various emergencies.

8. 泰国前十个月接待外国游客2546万人次 同比下降4.59%

中新社曼谷11月2日电 (梁峻祥 李映民)泰国旅游与体育部2日公布的统计数据显示，2026年1月至10月，泰国累计接待外国游客2546.43万人次，较去年同期减少4.59%。其中，中国游客累计达424.44万人次，同比增长12.44%。从客源市场看，中国继续位居泰国外国游客来源市场首位。马来西亚以322.65万人次位列第二，印度、俄罗斯和韩国分别以192.44万人次、141.43万人次和93.21万人次位列第三至第五位。

ENChina News Service, Bangkok, November 2 (Liang Junxiang, Li Yingmin) Statistics released by Thailand's Ministry of Tourism and Sports on the 2nd showed that from January to October 2026, Thailand received a total of 25.4643 million foreign tourists, a decrease of 4.59% from the same period last year. Among them, the total number of Chinese tourists reached 4.2444 million, a year-on-year increase of 12.44%. From the perspective of tourist source market, China continues to rank first in Thailand’s source market of foreign tourists. Malaysia ranked second with 3.2265 million passengers, and India, Russia and South Korea ranked third to fifth with 1.9244 million, 1.4143 million and 932,100 passengers respectively.
