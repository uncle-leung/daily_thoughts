5. 测控数据链系统助力中国10吨级电动商用飞机首次有人驾驶智能飞行
The measurement and control data link system helps China’s 10-ton electric commercial aircraft make its first manned intelligent flight

中新网北京11月15日电 (记者 孙自法)记者11月15日从中国电科获悉，在中国自主研制10吨级电动商用飞机CE‑25A 于11月14日完成的首次有人驾驶智能飞行中，中国电科网络通信研究院研制的测控数据链系统提供了可靠保障，实现CE‑25A飞行全程通信链路运行稳定、图像传输清晰，为后续常态化飞行开展奠定了坚实基础。中国电科网络通信研究院介绍，该测控数据链系统包含地面数据终端与机载数据终端，分别配置于地面指控站及飞机平台，可完成CE‑25A与地面指控站之间遥控遥测窄带数据、宽带任务载荷信息的双向传输，支撑地面指控站开展飞行状态监控和链路状态监控工作，具备通信距离远、带宽高、抗干扰能力强等技术优势。下一步，中国电科网络通信研究院项目团队将配合CE‑25A开展多场景常态化试飞验证，持续完成不同气象条件、飞行姿态下的链路稳定性试验，积累全维度飞行试验数据，完善有人机测控通信适配体系，为推动中国低空经济高质量发展、民用航空新能源技术创新提供有力支撑。据悉，作为中国商飞第一款电动商用飞机，CE-25A由中国商飞联合宁德时代、上海交通大学等国内合作伙伴共同研制，主要用于城市间商业飞行，可搭载19名乘客，最大起飞重量10吨，巡航速度300公里/小时，巡航高度2400米，航程400公里。

ENChina News Service, Beijing, November 15 (Reporter Sun Zifa) The reporter learned from China Electronics Technology on November 15 that during the first manned intelligent flight of the CE-25A, a 10-ton electric commercial aircraft independently developed by China, on November 14, the measurement and control data link system developed by China Electronics Network Communications Research Institute provided a reliable guarantee, achieving stable communication link operation and clear image transmission throughout the CE-25A flight, laying a solid foundation for subsequent normalized flights. According to the China Electronics Network Communications Research Institute, the measurement and control data link system includes ground data terminals and airborne data terminals, which are respectively configured on the ground control station and the aircraft platform. It can complete the two-way transmission of remote control telemetry narrowband data and broadband mission load information between the CE-25A and the ground control station. It supports the ground control station to carry out flight status monitoring and link status monitoring work. It has technical advantages such as long communication distance, high bandwidth, and strong anti-interference ability. In the next step, the China Electronics Network Communications Research Institute project team will cooperate with the CE-25A to carry out multi-scenario normal flight test verification, continue to complete link stability tests under different weather conditions and flight attitudes, accumulate full-dimensional flight test data, improve the manned and aircraft measurement and control communication adaptation system, and provide strong support for promoting the high-quality development of China's low-altitude economy and the innovation of new energy technologies in civil aviation. It is reported that as the first electric commercial aircraft of COMAC, the CE-25A was jointly developed by COMAC, CATL, Shanghai Jiao Tong University and other domestic partners. It is mainly used for commercial flights between cities. It can carry 19 passengers, has a maximum take-off weight of 10 tons, a cruising speed of 300 kilometers per hour, a cruising altitude of 2,400 meters, and a range of 400 kilometers.
