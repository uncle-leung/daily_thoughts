Japan’s defense budget may increase to 60 trillion yen in the next five years

据日本方面13日消息，多名相关人士透露，日本政府已开始协调，考虑将2027至2031财年防卫费总额定在60万亿日元，约合2.5万亿人民币。这一数字远高于2023至2027财年的43万亿日元。日本政府正在推进“安保三文件”修订，其中，新一轮覆盖2027至2031财年的《防卫力整备计划》，考虑将五年防卫费总额定为60万亿日元左右。该计划提到，未来五年防卫预算的重点是大规模采购无人机，以及为应对长期战争储备弹药和导弹。

ENAccording to news from Japan on the 13th, multiple relevant sources revealed that the Japanese government has begun coordination and is considering setting the total defense expenditure for the fiscal year 2027 to 2031 at 60 trillion yen, approximately 2.5 trillion yuan. This figure is much higher than the 43 trillion yen in fiscal year 2023 to 2027. The Japanese government is advancing the revision of the "Three Security Documents". Among them, a new round of the "Defense Force Preparation Plan" covering the fiscal year 2027 to 2031 is considering setting the total five-year defense cost at about 60 trillion yen. The plan mentioned that the defense budget for the next five years will focus on large-scale procurement of drones and stockpiling of ammunition and missiles for long-term war.

ENChina News Service, Washington, November 13 (Reporter Sha Hanting) The U.S. Federal Aviation Administration stated on the evening of the 13th local time that it had lifted the flight suspension order across the United States. The Federal Aviation Administration issued a directive that afternoon saying that due to communication failures at multiple air traffic control centers, flight departures across the United States were suspended. U.S. Transportation Secretary Duffy said at the White House that night that U.S. airspace had reopened at 20:15 Eastern Time, and flights would gradually resume. Airlines will take some time to resume normal operations, and "the recovery process will be slow."
