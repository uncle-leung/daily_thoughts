中新网北京10月28日电 (记者 孙自法)记者从中国地震局获悉，西藏自治区那曲市巴青县10月22日夜间发生的7.0级地震，近日首次获取到其地表破裂无人机高清影像数据。基于地表破裂带这一打开大地的“病历本”，地震系统通过前后方追踪调查和研究分析，已锁定巴青7.0级地震发震构造。巴青7.0级地震发生后，青海省地震局按照中国地震局统一部署派出现场工作队赶赴震区，同步开展地震烈度调查和地震流动监测等工作。青海省地震局现场工作队联合中国地震灾害防御中心、西藏自治区地震局等工作队，10月26日借助应急无人机装备，在青藏交界地区首次获取了本次地震同震地表破裂无人机高清影像数据。

ENChina News Service, Beijing, October 28 (Reporter Sun Zifa) The reporter learned from the China Earthquake Administration that a 7.0-magnitude earthquake occurred in Baqing County, Nagchu City, Tibet Autonomous Region on the night of October 22. Recently, high-definition drone image data of its surface rupture were obtained for the first time. Based on the surface rupture zone, which is a "medical record" that opens up the earth, the seismic system has identified the seismogenic structure of the 7.0-magnitude Baqing earthquake through front and rear tracking surveys and research and analysis. After the 7.0-magnitude Baqing earthquake occurred, the Qinghai Provincial Seismological Bureau dispatched an on-site team to the earthquake area in accordance with the unified deployment of the China Earthquake Administration to simultaneously carry out seismic intensity surveys and seismic flow monitoring. The on-site team of the Qinghai Provincial Earthquake Administration, in conjunction with the China Earthquake Disaster Prevention Center, the Tibet Autonomous Region Seismological Bureau and other teams, used emergency drone equipment to obtain high-definition drone high-definition image data of the co-seismic surface rupture of this earthquake in the Qinghai-Tibet border area for the first time on October 26.
