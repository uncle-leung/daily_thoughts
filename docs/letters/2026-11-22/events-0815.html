中新网马尼拉11月21日电 菲律宾卫生部20日发布报告称，菲律宾霍乱病例近期有所上升，10月11日至24日较此前两周增加69%。该部门正密切监测厄尔尼诺现象可能带来的饮水安全风险，呼吁公众注意饮用水和食品安全。菲律宾卫生部流行病学报告显示，10月11日至24日，菲律宾报告霍乱病例27例，较此前两周的16例增加69%。截至11月7日，今年累计报告霍乱病例551例，较2025年同期的1421例下降61%，病死率为2.18%。

ENManila, November 21 (Xinhua) – The Philippine Department of Health released a report on November 20 stating that cholera cases in the Philippines have recently risen, with a 69% increase from October 11 to 24 compared to the previous two weeks. The department is closely monitoring the potential risks to drinking water safety posed by the El Niño phenomenon and is urging the public to pay attention to drinking water and food safety. According to the Philippine Department of Health's epidemiological report, 27 cases of cholera were reported in the Philippines from October 11 to 24, representing a 69% increase from the 16 cases reported in the previous two weeks. As of November 7, a total of 551 cholera cases have been reported this year, representing a 61% decrease from the 1,421 cases reported during the same period in 2023. The case fatality rate stands at 2.18%.

7. 英国推进移民制度改革 持续关停庇护安置酒店

中新社伦敦11月20日电 (记者 欧阳开宇)英国内政部20日宣布，英国政府持续推进移民庇护制度改革，再度关停多处用于安置寻求庇护者的临时酒店，进一步缓解地方社区公共服务压力，压降庇护安置财政成本。据官方发布数据，自今年夏季以来，英国新关停8家庇护安置酒店，本年度累计关停数量达52家。目前英国仍在使用的庇护酒店约140家，较2023年峰值400家大幅缩减。酒店安置的庇护者人数较历史最高值下降超七成，安置规模显著回落。

ENLondon, November 20 (Xinhua) – The UK Home Office announced on November 20 that the British government is continuing to push forward with reforms to the asylum system. It has once again closed several temporary hotels used to house asylum seekers, further alleviating the strain on local community public services and reducing the financial costs of asylum accommodation. According to official data, since this summer, the UK has closed eight new asylum accommodation hotels, bringing the total number of closures this year to 52. Currently, there are approximately 140 asylum hotels still in use in the UK, a significant reduction from the peak of 400 in 2023. The number of asylum seekers accommodated in hotels has decreased by over 70% from its historical peak, marking a significant reduction in the scale of accommodation.

中新社约翰内斯堡11月20日电 (记者 孙翔)南非警察局20日公布的犯罪统计数据显示，今年7月至9月，南非共发生5590起谋杀案件，平均每天约有60人遭谋杀；其中，近3000起发生在公共场所。南非代理警察部长菲罗兹·卡查利亚当天在新闻发布会上表示，南非暴力犯罪总体呈下降趋势，较去年同期的5794起减少204起。本季度，持械抢劫案件出现显著下降。其中，汽车劫持案件同比下降16.8%，卡车劫持案件下降27.9%。

ENJohannesburg, November 20 (Xinhua) – According to crime statistics released by the South African Police Service on November 20 (Reporter: Sun Xiang), a total of 5,590 murders occurred in South Africa from July to September this year, averaging about 60 people murdered each day. Of these, nearly 3,000 took place in public spaces. At a press conference held on the same day, South Africa's Acting Minister of Police, Feroz Cachalia, stated that violent crime in South Africa is generally on a downward trend, with 204 fewer cases compared to the 5,794 cases reported during the same period last year. This quarter saw a significant decline in armed robbery cases. Specifically, car hijackings decreased by 16.8% year-on-year, and truck hijackings decreased by 27.9%.
