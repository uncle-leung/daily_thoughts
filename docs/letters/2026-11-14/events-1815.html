ENChina News Service, November 14. ENChina News Service, November 13. According to reports from Japanese media such as the Japan Broadcasting Association (NHK) and the Yomiuri Shimbun, bird flu epidemics have occurred in chicken farms in Kyoto Prefecture and Chiba Prefecture in Japan recently, and more than 200,000 chickens will be culled. According to reports, Chiba Prefecture said on the 13th that a highly pathogenic avian influenza virus was detected in a chicken farm in Asahi City, and the local area has begun to cull about 40,000 chickens raised in the chicken farm. Japan's Kyoto Prefecture said on the 12th that highly pathogenic avian influenza was confirmed in a chicken farm in Kameoka City, and about 200,000 chickens will be culled. The bird flu epidemic season in Japan is from autumn and winter to the following spring every year.
