6. 中国首款可互联中性原子量子计算机“乾阵”原型机在合肥发布
China's first interconnectable neutral atom quantum computer "Qianzhen" prototype was released in Hefei

中新社合肥11月8日电 (记者 张俊)中国首款可互联中性原子量子计算机“乾阵”原型机8日在安徽省合肥市发布。中性原子量子计算是当前量子计算的主流技术路线之一，该技术路线通过“光镊”阵列囚禁中性原子构建量子比特，具有扩展性强、运算保真度高、易于多机协同互联等特点，是实现通用量子计算的重要方向。“乾阵”原型机由合肥乾原量子科技有限公司(以下简称“乾原量子”)研制。据山西大学光电研究所教授、乾原量子董事长李刚介绍，“乾阵”原型机在架构设计之初就纳入互联技术，是中国首款具备多机互联能力的中性原子量子计算机原型机。

ENChina News Service, Hefei, November 8 (Reporter Zhang Jun) China's first interconnectable neutral atom quantum computer "Qianzhen" prototype was released in Hefei, Anhui Province on the 8th. Neutral atom quantum computing is one of the current mainstream technical routes for quantum computing. This technical route uses "optical tweezers" arrays to trap neutral atoms to construct qubits. It has the characteristics of strong scalability, high computational fidelity, and easy multi-machine collaborative interconnection. It is an important direction for realizing universal quantum computing. The prototype of "Qianzhen" was developed by Hefei Qianyuan Quantum Technology Co., Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "Qianyuan Quantum"). According to Li Gang, professor at the Institute of Optoelectronics of Shanxi University and chairman of Qianyuan Quantum, the "Qianzhen" prototype incorporated interconnection technology from the beginning of its architecture design and is China's first neutral atom quantum computer prototype with multi-machine interconnection capabilities.

中新社曼谷11月8日电 (王茜 李映民)泰国职业安全、职业健康与工作环境促进研究所所长南猜8日表示，气候变化导致全球气温升高、天气状况日趋不稳定，给劳动者健康和职业安全带来风险，其中户外工作者及高风险环境中的劳动者面临的威胁尤为突出。南猜指出，气候变化带来的劳动安全风险主要包括六个方面：极端高温、紫外线辐射、极端天气、空气污染、病媒传播疾病，以及其他间接健康与安全风险。持续高温可能导致劳动者出现热应激、热衰竭甚至中暑；长时间暴露于紫外线下，则可能损害皮肤和眼睛健康。在极端天气方面，暴雨、风暴、洪水和山体滑坡可能影响工作场所及通勤道路安全，增加工作事故风险。

ENChina News Service, Bangkok, November 8 (Wang Qi, Li Yingmin) Namchai, director of Thailand's Institute for Occupational Safety, Occupational Health and Work Environment Promotion, said on the 8th that climate change has led to rising global temperatures and increasingly unstable weather conditions, posing risks to workers' health and occupational safety. Among them, outdoor workers and workers in high-risk environments face particularly prominent threats. Namchai pointed out that the labor safety risks brought about by climate change mainly include six aspects: extreme high temperatures, ultraviolet radiation, extreme weather, air pollution, vector-borne diseases, and other indirect health and safety risks. Sustained high temperatures may cause workers to suffer from heat stress, heat exhaustion and even heatstroke; long-term exposure to ultraviolet rays may damage skin and eye health. In terms of extreme weather, heavy rains, storms, floods and landslides may affect workplace and commuter road safety and increase the risk of work accidents.
