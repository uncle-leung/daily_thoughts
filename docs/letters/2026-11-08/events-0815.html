2. 太平洋部分海域为何更蓝更绿？最新研究称或是微塑料浓度增加所致
Why are parts of the Pacific bluer and greener? The latest research says it may be caused by the increased concentration of microplastics

中新网北京11月8日电 (记者 孙自法)施普林格·自然旗下专业学术期刊《通讯-地球与环境》最新发表一篇环境研究论文称，研究人员通过分析卫星监测数据发现，2002至2022年间，在太平洋垃圾带内，部分太平洋海域的颜色似乎发生了变化，一些区域明显变得更蓝、更绿。究其原因，可能是海水中微塑料浓度增加所致。该论文介绍，直径小于300微米的微塑料颗粒造成的海洋污染难以直接测量，因为现有方法既费人力，也无法大规模应用。不过，实验室实验表明，这一尺寸的微塑料具有特定的光学特性，或能让人们通过遥感手段测量其在海水中的浓度。

ENChina News Service, Beijing, November 8 (Reporter Sun Zifa) A newly published environmental research paper in the professional academic journal "Communications-Earth and Environment" under Springer Nature stated that researchers analyzed satellite monitoring data and found that between 2002 and 2022, within the Pacific Garbage Patch, the color of some Pacific waters seems to have changed, with some areas becoming significantly bluer and greener. The reason may be due to the increased concentration of microplastics in seawater. According to the paper, marine pollution caused by microplastic particles less than 300 microns in diameter is difficult to directly measure because existing methods are labor-intensive and cannot be applied on a large scale. However, laboratory experiments have shown that microplastics of this size have specific optical properties that may allow their concentration in seawater to be measured remotely.
