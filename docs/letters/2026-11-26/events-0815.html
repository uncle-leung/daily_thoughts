5. 陕西立法保护千年蜀道 支持蜀道文化国际交流与文明互鉴
Shaanxi Legislates to Protect the Ancient Shu Road, Supporting International Exchanges and Mutual Learning of Cultures Related to the Shu Road

中新网西安11月26日电 (记者 杨英琦)25日，陕西省第十四届人民代表大会常务委员会第二十六次会议表决通过《陕西省蜀道保护条例》(以下简称《条例》)，自2027年3月1日起施行。蜀道，又称秦蜀古道，《条例》明确为1937年川陕公路通车前，中国历史上形成的以关中地区为起点，翻越秦岭、巴山连接巴蜀地区，具有多重价值的道路系统。据悉，陕西是蜀道的起始中枢，境内分布子午道、褒斜道等七条主线，线路总长约1300公里，沿线摩崖碑刻、古村落遗存数量庞大，文物资源密集，历史价值突出。《条例》共四十七条，明确蜀道保护应遵循保护第一、传承优先、加强管理、合理利用的原则，维护蜀道历史形态、风貌的真实性和完整性。

ENXi'an, November 26 (Xinhua) – On November 25, the 26th session of the Standing Committee of the 14th Shaanxi Provincial People's Congress voted to adopt the "Regulations on the Protection of the Shaanxi Section of the Ancient Sichuan Road" (hereinafter referred to as the "Regulations"), which will come into effect on March 1, 2027. The Shu Road, also known as the Ancient Qin-Shu Road, is defined in the Regulation as a road system of multifaceted significance that originated in the Guanzhong region, traversed the Qinling and Bashan Mountains, and connected the Bashu region. It was established throughout Chinese history prior to the opening of the Sichuan-Shaanxi Highway in 1937. It is understood that Shaanxi is the starting point of the Shu Road. Within its borders are seven main routes, including the Ziwu Road and the Baoxie Road, with a total length of approximately 1,300 kilometers. Along these routes, there are a vast number of cliffside inscriptions and ancient village remains, with a high concentration of cultural relics and significant historical value. The Regulation consists of forty-seven articles, which clearly state that the protection of the Shu Roads should adhere to the principles of prioritizing protection, giving precedence to cultural heritage, strengthening management, and ensuring rational use, in order to preserve the authenticity and integrity of the historical form and character of the Shu Roads.
