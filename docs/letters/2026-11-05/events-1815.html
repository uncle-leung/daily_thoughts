ENChina News Service, Nanning, November 5 (Reporter Yang Chen) The reporter learned from the Nanning Communication Center (hereinafter referred to as the Nanning Communication Center) of the South China Sea Navigation Support Center of the Ministry of Transport of China on the 5th that the center has recently completed the overlay of the Pinglu Canal electronic river map, ship positioning and other information onto a portable pilot terminal, realizing an integrated display of the Pinglu Canal channel environment and ship navigation information. It is reported that, focusing on the Pinglu Canal pilotage business needs, Nanning Communication Center undertook the portable pilot terminal (PPU) application construction project carried out by the Guangxi Pilot Center. On this basis, it completed the development of intelligent pilot services based on the S-100 standard, further enriching the navigation support service functions of the portable pilot terminal. S-100 is a new generation general maritime data model standard proposed by the International Hydrographic Organization (IHO). It can superimpose information layers related to navigation safety such as water level, water depth, navigation rules, etc. on standard electronic charts to realize linked calculations and automatic push of navigation aid information. According to the International Maritime Organization (IMO) schedule, newly installed electronic chart display and information systems (ECDIS) must meet the S-100 standard from January 1, 2029.
