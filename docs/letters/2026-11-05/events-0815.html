ENThe State Administration for Market Regulation recently issued the "Regulations on Administrative Punishment Discretion for Market Supervision and Management", which will come into effect on January 1 next year. This new regulation directly addresses the discretionary power in market supervision administrative penalties: for the first time, it distinguishes between lighter penalties and reduced penalties, and unifies the identification standards for common discretionary factors such as "first violation". In the past, for the same type of illegal acts, the punishment results may be quite different in different regions and in the hands of different law enforcement officials. "Colleagues are punished differently" has always been a problem that business entities have focused on. The new regulations are to engrave a uniform scale on the "ruler" of law enforcement, so that every punishment is proportionate and lenient and strict.

中新社巴黎11月4日电(记者 李洋)法国卢浮宫摄影特展当地时间4日拉开帷幕，聚焦卢浮宫在摄影史和摄影艺术中的独特地位。该展览是法国文化部纪念摄影术诞生200周年系列活动的重要组成部分。据介绍，该展览展出约300幅摄影作品，既有摄影先驱的杰作，也有现代摄影师的探索性创作。这些照片涵盖卢浮宫馆藏艺术品、展厅、参观者、工作人员以及卢浮宫的隐秘区域等诸多主题，为人们审视卢浮宫提供了全新视角。

ENChina News Service, Paris, November 4 (Reporter Li Yang) A special photography exhibition at the Louvre Museum in France kicked off on the 4th local time, focusing on the unique position of the Louvre Museum in the history of photography and photographic art. The exhibition is an important part of the French Ministry of Culture’s series of activities commemorating the 200th anniversary of the birth of photography. According to reports, the exhibition displays about 300 photographic works, including masterpieces by pioneers of photography and exploratory creations by modern photographers. These photos cover many topics such as the Louvre’s collections of artworks, exhibition halls, visitors, staff, and hidden areas of the Louvre, providing a new perspective for people to view the Louvre.

6. 泰国总理提出“重塑泰国”构想 强调增强经济自主能力
The Prime Minister of Thailand proposed the concept of "reshaping Thailand" and emphasized enhancing economic independence.

中新社曼谷11月4日电 (王茜 李映民)泰国总理阿努廷4日在曼谷举行的2026年泰国标准经济论坛上发表主旨演讲，提出“重塑泰国”构想，强调泰国应立足现有产业和发展基础，通过调整经济结构、提升产业竞争力，增强应对全球经济、技术和地缘政治变化的能力。阿努廷表示，“重塑泰国”并非从零开始建设，而是对现有资源和产业进行调整、改进和提升。他认为，增强自主能力和国家韧性是泰国应对外部不确定性的重要方向，能源、粮食、国防工业及公共行政等领域都需要加强自身保障能力。在经济发展方面，阿努廷提出培育新的经济增长动力，重点发展现代农业、食品技术、国防工业、半导体和数据中心等产业，同时推动法律法规改革，减少不必要的行政审批程序。

ENChina News Service, Bangkok, November 4 (Wang Qi, Li Yingmin) Thai Prime Minister Anutin delivered a keynote speech at the 2026 Thailand Standard Economic Forum held in Bangkok on the 4th, proposing the concept of "reshaping Thailand" and emphasizing that Thailand should be based on existing industries and development foundations, adjust the economic structure, enhance industrial competitiveness, and enhance its ability to respond to global economic, technological and geopolitical changes. Anutin said that "reshaping Thailand" does not mean building from scratch, but adjusting, improving and upgrading existing resources and industries. He believes that enhancing independent capabilities and national resilience is an important direction for Thailand to cope with external uncertainties. Energy, food, defense industry, public administration and other fields need to strengthen their own security capabilities. In terms of economic development, Anutin proposed cultivating new economic growth drivers, focusing on the development of modern agriculture, food technology, defense industry, semiconductors and data centers and other industries, while promoting the reform of laws and regulations and reducing unnecessary administrative approval procedures.
