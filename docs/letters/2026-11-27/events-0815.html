3. 《生态质量评价标准（试行）》将于今年12月1日起实施
The "Ecological Quality Assessment Standards (Trial)" will come into effect on December 1st of this year.

中新网11月27日电 11月27日，生态环境部召开11月例行新闻发布会，生态环境部新闻发言人裴晓菲介绍，近日，生态环境部发布《生态质量评价标准(试行)》，将于今年12月1日起实施。裴晓菲表示，为评价区域生态质量状况和保护成效，近日，生态环境部发布《生态质量评价标准(试行)》(以下简称《标准》)，将于今年12月1日起实施。《标准》的发布将进一步健全生态保护与修复监管体系，为美丽中国建设成效考核提供技术支撑。《标准》紧扣提升生态系统多样性、稳定性、持续性的战略要求，在指标体系方面，整合优化《生态环境状况评价技术规范》、《区域生物多样性评价标准》和《区域生态质量评价办法(试行)》，综合考虑数据可得性、区域差异和年际变化等因素，从生态系统多样性、稳定性、持续性三个方面，设置自然生态系统面积占比、生境连通度、生态空间人为活动影响强度等8项基本指标。

ENChina News Service, November 27 – On November 27, the Ministry of Ecology and Environment held its regular November press conference. Ministry of Ecology and Environment spokesperson Pei Xiaofei announced that the Ministry of Ecology and Environment recently released the "Ecological Quality Assessment Standards (Trial)," which will come into effect on December 1 of this year. Pei Xiaofei stated that in order to assess the state of regional ecological quality and the effectiveness of conservation efforts, the Ministry of Ecology and Environment recently issued the "Ecological Quality Assessment Standards (Trial)" (hereinafter referred to as the "Standards"), which will come into effect on December 1 of this year. The release of the Standard will further improve the regulatory system for ecological protection and restoration, providing technical support for the performance evaluation of the Beautiful China initiative. The "Standards" are closely aligned with the strategic requirements of enhancing the diversity, stability, and sustainability of ecosystems. In terms of the indicator system, they integrate and optimize the "Technical Guidelines for Ecological and Environmental Status Assessment," the "Regional Biodiversity Assessment Standards," and the "Regional Ecological Quality Assessment Methods (Trial)." They comprehensively consider factors such as data availability, regional differences, and interannual variations. From the three aspects of ecosystem diversity, stability, and sustainability, they establish eight basic indicators, including the proportion of natural ecosystem area, habitat connectivity, and the intensity of human activity impact on ecological spaces.

ENAt today's (November 27) regular press conference for November, a relevant official from the Ministry of Ecology and Environment stated that, in response to issues with local ecological and environmental standards, a document has been jointly issued with the State Administration for Market Regulation to establish a systematic framework. Local ecological and environmental standards, formulated based on local environmental endowments and industrial characteristics, have played an important role in promoting the improvement of regional environmental quality and driving the green upgrading of industries. With the rapid development of local pollutant discharge standards, some new issues have gradually emerged: The development of standards across different regions is uneven. Some provinces with clusters of key industries only implement national standards and lack targeted local standards, making it difficult to meet local environmental improvement requirements. In some regions with a large number of local standards, the standards formulated for different industries, river basins, and pollutants overlap with each other, making them difficult for grassroots law enforcement and enterprises to implement. There is insufficient alignment of standards across provinces, and different regions have varying control requirements for similar emission sources within the same river basin or region. Recently, the Ministry of Ecology and Environment and the State Administration for Market Regulation jointly issued the "Opinions on Effectively Implementing Local Ecological and Environmental Standards." These opinions focus on these prominent issues and propose ten measures from three aspects: system development, quality improvement, and mechanism enhancement.
