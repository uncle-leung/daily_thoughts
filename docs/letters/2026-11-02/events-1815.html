7. 三部门发文完善设施农用地管理，以正面清单区分用地方式
The three departments issued a document to improve the management of facility agricultural land and use a positive list to distinguish land use methods

中新网北京11月2日电 (记者 庞无忌)自然资源部2日消息，近日，自然资源部、农业农村部、国家林业和草原局联合印发《关于完善设施农用地管理的通知》(以下简称《通知》)，采取正面清单方式区分设施农业用地方式，并从设施农用地选址、规模、备案、登记、监管等方面完善举措。《通知》要求区分设施农业用地方式，通过“设施农业使用土地正面清单”实行差异化管理。未对耕作层造成破坏的设施农业等按原土地用途管理，无需办理用地手续；对耕作层造成破坏、直接为农业生产服务的生产和附属设施等按设施农用地管理，办理设施农用地备案手续。未纳入“设施农业使用土地正面清单”的设施，办理建设用地审批手续。

ENChina News Service, Beijing, November 2 (Reporter Pang Wuji) The Ministry of Natural Resources reported on the 2nd that recently, the Ministry of Natural Resources, the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs, and the National Forestry and Grassland Administration jointly issued the "Notice on Improving the Management of Facility Agricultural Land" (hereinafter referred to as the "Notice"), adopting a positive list to distinguish the methods of facility agricultural land, and improving measures in terms of site selection, scale, filing, registration, and supervision of facility agricultural land. The "Notice" requires distinguishing the land use methods for facility agriculture and implementing differentiated management through the "Positive List of Land Used by Facility Agriculture". Facilitated agriculture that does not cause damage to the farming layer shall be managed according to the original land use, and there is no need to go through land use procedures; production and ancillary facilities that cause damage to the farming layer and directly serve agricultural production shall be managed as facility agricultural land, and registration procedures for facility agricultural land shall be completed. Facilities that are not included in the "positive list of land used for facility agriculture" must go through the construction land approval procedures.
